from conda-forge (the exact files are listed with their SHA-256 in packaging/appimage/qt.lock of the
sources) and KDE Kirigami, KCoreAddons, KI18n and KPipeWire built from their sources. The text of each licence is in qt/share/licenses
of the installed copy (~/.local/lib/reiwand/current/qt/share/licenses for the AppImage,
/usr/lib64/reiwand/qt/share/licenses for the RPM). Only the libraries the programs load are carried.
