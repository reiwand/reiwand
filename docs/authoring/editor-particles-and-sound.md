# Particles and sound in the editor

How to create and adjust particle systems, sound layers and links that follow the sound, with the scene editor's tools. To open the editor, the canvas and the inspector, see [editor](editor.md) and [editor-canvas](editor-canvas.md). For the data format, see [particles-and-sound](particles-and-sound.md). The terminal actions are in [actions](actions.md).

Particles and sound in the editor, by task:

- [Particle systems and emitters](editor-particles-and-sound/systems-and-emitters.md): adding systems, shapes, emitting from layers, rates and bursts.
- [How particles start and the forces](editor-particles-and-sound/starts-and-forces.md): control points, starting values, colors, noise, remaps, pulls, flocks and force limits.
- [Colliders, trails and the look](editor-particles-and-sound/colliders-and-look.md): walls, ropes and trails, the look, drawing order, child systems and cost.
- [Sound layers and links](editor-particles-and-sound/sound.md): adding a sound, its waveform, fades, triggers, volume and links to bands.
- [Sound levels and shortcuts](editor-particles-and-sound/sound-response.md): levels, the response, smoothing, pointer links and keyboard shortcuts.
