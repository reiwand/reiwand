# Lights, 3D, camera and depth

These pages gather everything that gives a scene relief: lights that illuminate flat images, 3D
models, shadows, a camera that moves among them, and ways to give depth to a flat image. The
examples come from [`examples/lights`](examples/lights/scene.json),
[`examples/model-3d`](examples/model-3d/scene.json) and
[`examples/depth`](examples/depth/scene.json), and they pass `reiwand-ctl pack check`.

Layers and common fields are in [layers.md](layers.md). Meshes with bones (images bent by a
skeleton) are in [meshes.md](meshes.md).

3D, by topic:

- [Lights on flat layers](3d/flat-lights.md): lighting layers that are not models.
- [3D models](3d/models.md): models in a scene.
- [Lights, shadows and the camera](3d/lights-and-camera.md): lights among models, shadows and the 3D camera.
- [Depth and bright colors](3d/depth-and-hdr.md): depth for flat layers, HDR and tone mapping.

## Not covered here

- Meshes, bones, clips, inverse kinematics and physics: [meshes.md](meshes.md).
- Own material shaders: [effects.md](effects.md).
- Using the 3D editor (handles, authoring view): [editor.md](editor.md) and
  [actions.md](actions.md).
