# Editor: 3D, lights, camera and materials

These pages explain how to put 3D models in a scene, move them, light them and animate them from the scene editor. For the canvas and the inspector see [editor-canvas](editor-canvas.md); for the skeleton of a flat mesh see [editor-drawing](editor-drawing.md); for the keys and clips of the timeline see [editor-animation](editor-animation.md). The arguments of each terminal action are in [actions](actions.md).

Almost everything on these pages appears in the **Inspector** panel (on the right). Some parts appear when you choose a layer and others when you choose the canvas itself.

| What you want to touch | What you choose |
| --- | --- |
| A model, its materials, its animation | The **3D model** layer |
| A light | The light layer |
| Lights of the whole scene, ambient light, shadows, 3D camera, paths, bright colors, cost | The canvas (the root of the scene) |
| How an image, a video or a text receives light | That layer, **Lighting** part |

3D in the editor, by task:

- [Models and moving in 3D](editor-3d/models.md): adding models, 3D groups, the handles and the view.
- [The camera and lights](editor-3d/camera-and-lights.md): the scene camera, camera paths, lights and projected images.
- [Light, shadows and materials](editor-3d/light-and-materials.md): volumetric, ambient and sky light, lit layers, shadows and materials.
- [3D animation and cost](editor-3d/animation-and-cost.md): animating models, bones, bright colors and what 3D costs.
