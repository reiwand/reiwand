On this page: engine, time, props, audio, input and scene.

The reiwand interface#

Everything comes from import { ... } from "reiwand".

engine: the state of the scene#

Field What it gives
time, dt The scene time and what has passed since the previous frame, in seconds.
daytime The local time of day, from 0 (midnight) to almost 1.
fps, quality The frame rate and the quality level (0 to 3).
locale, language The language chosen among those of the package (including those of its text layers), as a valid language tag (de, en-US); en-US if the package has none.
canvas, screen [width, height] of the canvas and of the screen, without output names.
device desktop or mobile.
mode wallpaper, lockscreen, screensaver, editor or preview.
orientation landscape or portrait.

time.format(pattern, date)

Gives the date (that of date, or now) written with the letters of the date values of scene.json, in the language of engine.locale: time.format("HH:mm"), time.format("EEEE d MMMM").

props: the properties the user chooses#

Function What it gives
props.get(id) The current value. A chosen file or folder gives "wpak-user://<id>", never the path, and null while there is none.
props.all() All the values, in one object.
props.label(id) The text of the chosen option of a combo, in the language of whoever uses the wallpaper.
props.color(id) A color as { r, g, b, a } from 0 to 1.
props.pressed(id) true if that button was pressed since the previous frame.

A script never writes properties. If it wants something to change with a property, it reads it in update or in properties and changes the layers.

audio: the sound#

Part What it gives
bass, mid, treble, level Levels from 0 to 1.
beat true on the frame of a beat.
bands(n, channel) A new list of n bands (16, 32 or 64) of the channel "left", "right" or "average" (the default).
buffers(n) { left, right, average } as Float32Arrays of n bands that the scene fills by itself before each frame. Ask for them once, outside update.

Without sound, or without the user allowing it, everything is 0.

input: the pointer#

input.cursor has x and y (fractions of the screen, from 0 to 1), inside (the pointer is on the screen), buttons and left (the left button is pressed), scene ([x, y] in canvas pixels) and screen ([x, y] in screen pixels). buttons and left are 0 without the clicks input. When the pointer leaves, it goes smoothly back to the center in about 0.15 s.

scene: the layers and the stage#

Function What it does
scene.layer(id) The handle of a layer. It is always the same object for the same id.
scene.layers() The ids of the top-level layers, in drawing order.
scene.spawn(template, options) Adds a copy of a template from templates (declare layers).
scene.destroy(id) Removes a copy that a script made.
scene.order(id, index) Moves a top-level layer; 0 is the background.

scene.spawn puts the copy on top of everything, or at options.at. Its id is options.id (letters, digits, _ and -, up to 64) or <template>_<n>. The other options are fields of the layer (position, opacity...). The children of a copied group are called <id>.<child>. Going over 256 copies, or over 512 layers in total, throws an error.

The fields of a layer#

They are read and written by name: layer.opacity = 0.5.

Field Value
position, origin, skew [x, y]; skew in degrees, up to ±89
scale, parallax [x, y] or a single number
rotation degrees, clockwise
opacity 0 to 1
visible true or false
color [r, g, b, a] from 0 to 1, { r, g, b, a } or "#rrggbb"
size [width, height] of images, videos, solids and spectra; the size in points of a text
text, font, align the text (up to 4096 characters), the font (one of the scene, or sans-serif, serif, monospace) and left, center or right; the text a script sets applies in all languages, and when reading it from a layer with per-language text you receive that of the language being shown

Vectors accept lists, { x, y } or those of reiwand/math. Reading a field gives what the script wrote or, if it wrote nothing, what the scene computed on the previous frame. What you write lasts until you write it again or call layer.reset("field") (layer.reset() releases them all). The scene clamps what it receives: positions and sizes to 100,000 pixels, scales to 1000, origin and parallax to ±10, opacity and colors to 0..1.

Other members of a layer:

Member What it is
id, name, type, initial Its identifier, its type and the layer as scene.json writes it.
on(event, function) Handles click, down, up, enter, leave and move on solid layers or hit bones (below).
effect(index or name) An effect of the layer (declare effects).
video, anim(clip) Control of videos and clips (declare animations).
parent, children, world, setParent(id, keep) The hierarchy (declare hierarchy).
bones, bone(name), shape(name, weight), impulse(bone, [vx, vy]), hit(x, y), attachment(name) Bones, blend shapes and anchors of a mesh or model layer (declare animations). In a model, positions are in pixels of its box and the bone keeps its depth.
destroy() Removes the layer if it is a copy that a script made.

Effects. layer.effect(0) or layer.effect("glow") (the name is put in uses, [a-z0-9_]{1,32}) gives enabled, set(parameter, value), get(parameter), reset(), index, name and effect. set accepts numbers, vectors, colors, switches, blend modes and lists of emitters (up to 64 { from, to, angle, strength }), checks them against the description of the effect and clamps them to its range. The images of an effect cannot be changed. What you set lasts until you change it.

Pointer events. layer.on("click", (e) => ...) receives { type, layer, local, scene, bone }: local is pixels of the layer from its top-left corner, scene canvas pixels and bone the bone touched (null if none). enter, leave and move follow the topmost solid layer under the pointer. down goes to that layer, which also receives move and up until all buttons are released; click follows an up over the layer that received the down. They arrive on the next frame. enter, leave and move ask for the pointer input; click, down and up, clicks. Clicks are off on each screen until the user turns them on, and they come only from the compositor or the desktop, never from another program.

Video. layer.video (declare animations) has play(), pause(), stop() (goes back to start), seek(seconds), time, duration, playing, rate (0 to 16), volume (0 to 1) and on("ended", function).

Clips. layer.anim(clip) controls the animation layers of a mesh layer that touch that clip: play(), pause(), stop(), seek(seconds), rate (0 to 16), weight (0 to 1), time, duration, playing and on("ended", function).

Hierarchy. With hierarchy, layer.parent is the id of the layer it follows (or null), layer.children the ids of those that follow it and layer.world a Mat3 of reiwand/math from the pixels of the layer to those of the canvas. layer.setParent(id, keep) makes it follow another (null for none); with keep the layer stays where it is. A layer cannot follow itself or follow one that follows it, or have more than 8 chained parents.

The stage#

With camera (and bloom for the glow): scene.camera (center, zoom, rotation, shake: { enabled, amplitude, roughness, speed }), scene.parallax (amount, delay, pointer, offset), scene.bloom (threshold, strength, radius, tint), scene.camera3d (fov, eye, center, up) and the colors scene.background, scene.ambient and scene.sky. They are read as the scene left them in canvas plus what the script wrote. Writing an object changes only the fields it names, within the ranges of canvas; writing null gives the field back to the scene. The camera shake stands still with the reduce-motion option and at level Q0.

Custom shapes#

With geometry and a pools in scene.json: scene.mesh(pool) gives a set of shapes with create({ vertices, indices, format, visible }), shapes and clear(); each shape has update, destroy, id and alive. vertices is a Float32Array or a list of numbers in pixels of the set, with format "xy", "xyuv" (the default), "xyrgba" or "xyuvrgba".
