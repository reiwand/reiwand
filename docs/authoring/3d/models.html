3D models

A model layer draws a binary glTF model (.glb) with metal and roughness materials. On its own, it is drawn in its own box (size, the canvas by default) with depth and then composited like any layer, with its effects, masks, blending and transform. There are up to 16 mesh and model layers in a scene.

.glb files are made with common 3D tools (FBX and OBJ are converted to .glb). The program reads the headers and the JSON part once and rejects models that load outside files or that ask for extensions other than KHR_materials_emissive_strength, KHR_materials_unlit, KHR_mesh_quantization and KHR_texture_transform. They can have up to 2,000,000 triangles and 256 bones per skeleton. The names of nodes (attachment points), joints (bones), meshes (parts), materials, blend shapes and animations are read from the file, and every name you put in the scene is checked against them. To see what a model contains:

reiwand-ctl scene import MY-DIR model.glb --id model
reiwand-ctl scene ask MY-DIR model_info '{"asset":"model"}'

It reports the triangles, the materials, the textures, the bones, the clips and which equipment categories can handle it.

Field Prefill What it does
model required The .glb asset.
position, rotation, scale Three numbers each (or a single one for all three), in model units and degrees (it is rotated around x, then y, then z). They can follow values.
size the canvas The box in pixels.
env A panoramic (equirectangular) image that the materials reflect: blurry for the surrounding light and sharper for the shiny parts.
materials By material name from the file, up to 64: see below.
clips, animations, ik, physics, rules, shapes, expressions, hit, root_motion Animation of the model. See Meshes, bones and animation.

Materials#

The materials in the file give the color, the metal, the roughness, the normal, the occlusion and the glow with their factors. A scene material multiplies its color by color, replaces the glow with emissive, chooses shader and changes the rest:

Field What it does
shader pbr (default), unlit, fur, foliage, chroma, or the id of an own effect with stage: "material".
color, emissive Multiplies the color; replaces the glow.
lit false draws it without light.
double_sided, cull Both faces, and which face is hidden: back, front or none.
blend opaque, mask, blend or add. blend or an opacity below 1 draws it after the opaque things, from far to near.
depth_test, depth_write How it treats depth.
cast_shadows, receive_shadows Casts and receives shadows (both true by default).
maps Replaces maps from the file with images from the scene: base, orm, normal, occlusion, emissive. Those of orm, normal and occlusion must be linear.
params Shader parameters.

Shader parameters:

  • pbr: metallic, roughness, occlusion (0 to 1) and normal_scale (0 to 10).
  • fur: layers (1 to 32, 12 by default: layers of fur, which drop at low quality levels), length (0 to 100, 10 by default, in hundredths of the model's radius), density (0 to 1, 0.5 by default), and comb_x, comb_y, comb_z (-1 to 1) to comb it; maps.comb is a linear image that combs each spot on its own.
  • foliage (foliage that sways with canvas.wind): strength (0 to 10, 1) and speed (0 to 10, 1). The red of the file's vertex colors says how much each vertex moves. Without wind it stays still and does not ask for frames.
  • chroma: makes colors close to key transparent (green by default), with threshold (0.4) and softness (0.1), from 0 to 1.
  • An own effect with stage: "material" (see Effects) defines fn material(s: Surface) -> Surface, which runs for every pixel before the light. It is compiled separately from the drawing, with the same 10-second limit as the other effects: if it cannot be compiled or takes longer, the model is shown with pbr and the editor reports it among what was left out, without the wallpaper stalling in the meantime.

A material is changed by name from the terminal with reiwand-ctl scene act DIR scene3d.

3D groups#

A group with space: "3d" draws all its models at once with a shared depth, in a box the size of the canvas, and is composited as a single layer in the group's place. A 3D group inside another shares its depth if it is "simple" (no effects, mask, blending, opacity, color, brightness, clip, tilt, z or depth_test).

Flat layers inside a 3D group are drawn in front of the models, in their order, unless they set depth_test: then they are on a plane transform.z pixels from where the camera looks and the models in front of them cover them. Up to 4 distinct planes; more layers share the nearest one. Particle layers count as flat; with perspective each particle hides pixel by pixel behind the models, also if they are lit, refract, have their own shader, trails or ropes (each point at the depth of its particle), and child and grandchild systems likewise. Those that refract hide their distortion behind the nearest models, but next to the edge of one they may drag some of its pixels. At the lowest quality the whole layer goes on its plane.

A model inside a 3D group with opacity below 1 is drawn transparent, blended in linear light after the opaque ones, from far to near. That is why it does not fade the same way as a standalone model, and it casts no shadow.

What it costs#

Each model adds to the scene's cost one unit per 200,000 triangles on a dedicated computer GPU (measured on such a card) and per 60,000 on phones and the more modest computers, which have not been measured yet; the editor and the check warn with the cautious count. The same is counted again for each shadow map it is drawn into (the first two lights with shadows; a point light draws six). That count does not drop with the quality level (at t2 only one light is drawn), and each model also pays a fixed cost per stage, so a scene with many small models is counted as more expensive than it really is. reiwand-ctl scene bench --passes shows how long each pass of a 3D group takes: shadow maps, depth for particles, models and second camera. The equipment categories admit up to 50,000 (t0), 150,000 (t1), 500,000 (t2) and 2,000,000 (t3) triangles, skeletons of up to 256 bones and shadows from 0, 0, 1 and 2 lights. A category shows the poster of a scene whose models draw more triangles than it admits at the lowest level; other heavy scenes run there at lower quality. A model's textures can be PNG, JPEG or WebP of up to 4096 pixels (2048 on phones and in categories t0 and t1).

reiwand-ctl scene ask MY-DIR cost3d '{"quality":"q2"}'
reiwand-ctl scene bench my.wpak --tier t1

A model whose values do not move is drawn once and stored; a 3D group is only redrawn when a model, the camera, a light, the surrounding light or the size changes, so a still scene does not ask for frames.
