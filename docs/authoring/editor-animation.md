# Animation in the editor: timelines, keys, curves and clips

These pages explain how to make a scene's values change over time: timelines, keyframes, the curve editor, bone and model clips, video clocks and the effects' rest rules.

The strip at the bottom of the editor, below the canvas, is the time area. It shows the scene's timelines. When you choose a clip of a skeleton or of a model, the same strip changes to show that clip's bones (see [How to edit a clip](editor-animation/clips-and-videos.md#how-to-edit-a-clip-of-a-skeleton-or-a-model)). For the rest of the editor see [editor](editor.md), [canvas](editor-canvas.md) and [drawing](editor-drawing.md). The terminal actions are in [actions](actions.md).

Animation in the editor, by task:

- [Keys and the time strip](editor-animation/keys.md): time, keys, the playhead, curves between keys and timelines.
- [Timelines, triggers and curves](editor-animation/timelines.md): adjusting timelines, events, triggers and the curve editor.
- [Clips and videos](editor-animation/clips-and-videos.md): clips of skeletons and models, their keys, events and blending, and videos.
- [Sprites, rest rules and shortcuts](editor-animation/sprites-and-rest.md): sprite sheets, when a scene wakes up and the shortcut summary.
