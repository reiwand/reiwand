# Canvas, layers and inspector of the editor

These pages explain how to place and adjust a scene's layers with the mouse and with the inspector: the canvas, the layer list, the inspector, templates, shared parts and assets.

In the **Scene** layout the editor window has three areas. On the left, a panel with tabs: **Assets** and **Scripts**. In the center, the canvas, under its **Canvas** tab, with its toolbar and, below it, the time area (see [animation](editor-animation.md)) next to the **Problems** tab. On the right, the **Inspector** tab and, below it, **Layers**. The other layouts place the same panels for other kinds of work (see [layouts](editor/opening.md#layouts)). Each panel can be resized, moved to another group or closed, and the menus of the top bar (**File**, **Edit**, **Layer**, **View** and **Window**) do what the panels and bars do (see [editor](editor/opening.md#what-the-window-looks-like)). The rest of the editor is in [editor](editor.md). The terminal actions are in [actions](actions.md).

The canvas and the layers, by task:

- [Choosing and moving layers](editor-canvas/choosing-and-moving.md): choosing layers, moving, scaling, skewing, rotating, tilting and fitting them.
- [The view and the canvas](editor-canvas/view-and-canvas.md): handles, zoom, locking and hiding, keys, canvas size, glow, parallax, the pointer and a 2D camera.
- [Adding and arranging layers](editor-canvas/layers.md): adding, grouping, ordering, renaming, duplicating and parenting layers.
- [The inspector, templates and assets](editor-canvas/inspector-and-assets.md): the inspector, pointer input, templates, assets packages and managing files.
