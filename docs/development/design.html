How Reiwand looks and why. This page is for contributors who change the desktop app (QML), the phone app (Android, Kotlin) or the pages the server renders. The token tables are in Look of the desktop app and the way the tones are derived is in Interface palette; this page does not repeat them. It gives the rules and says which piece to use.

The idea#

The window is the wallpaper you have on. The app is a title screen: a few confident words and a shelf of large covers over the living picture. The interface is always dark (the editor alone may be light, see Workbench). Every tone has a name and comes from the wallpaper in use, except four fixed ones. There is no blur, no glass and no decorative gradient: separation is a veil in the picture's own deepest tone or an opaque panel in that tone.

  • Menus, titles and the main action in Red Hat Display; running text in Onest; icons are Phosphor.
  • One lit thing per view. The accent is scarce. State is words plus icon plus colour.
  • Flat tonal layers with soft dark shadows, not outlines around everything.
  • The voice is the user's: words, not walls of options. The technical part sits behind "Details", "More" or "Advanced".

The three surfaces#

All three share the tone names, the two typefaces, the Phosphor icons, the 6 px radius, the 4/8/12/16 spacing rhythm, the one-lit-thing rule, the three state tones, the main button in the accent and the Frost keyboard focus ring.

  • Desktop (apps/reiwand-app). Three kinds of window, all full-bleed:
    • Title screens (TitleFrame: Library, Explore, Lists, Sources, Create): menu words on the left over a veil, the wallpaper's title large at the top right, and a lower band with the type filter, the search and the shelf of covers. The focused cover is larger and fully lit.
    • Options screens (OptionsFrame: Settings, options of a wallpaper, list, source, Account): the picture shrinks to the strip of the menu and an opaque Night panel holds ListRows, with sections and the key hints at its foot.
    • Work surface (WorkFrame: editor, review, publish): the picture becomes the canvas, the sides are Night panels. Authors only, desktop only.
    • Small surfaces (SmallPanel: tray, menu of one control, question, confirmation) are never a page; they open next to the control that opens them.
  • Phone (android/app/src/main/kotlin). User functions only, never authoring. The wallpaper is full screen, the title and controls sit in the upper third and the shelf sits above three tabs (Tabs: Library, Lists, Settings). Type follows the system text size, touch targets are 48 dp or more, text is 14 sp or more, Sheets replace small panels, and there are no keyboard hints and no hover.
  • Server (apps/reiwand-server). Pages are plain HTML and one column (--column, 46 rem). There is no wallpaper in use: the head of the page (.band) takes the latest published cover under a veil, or the plain night ground (.band.bare, f-none) while the repository is empty. Sizes are in rem and fluid, nothing is icon-only, a confirmation is a state of the page and there are no keyboard hints.

Colours#

One deep family, one light family, one accent per wallpaper, four fixed colours. The default set below is what the code uses when no wallpaper gives its own (Look.qml, Tones.NONE on the phone, :root in style.css).

Tone Hex Use
Night #0a1322 Page ground, panels, veils, text on lit rows and on the accent
High night #121e33 Buttons at rest, fields, notices, key caps, second-level panels
Night line #2a3a57 1 px lines, rail tracks, pressed quiet button
Mist #b9c4d6 Second-level text, captions, resting menu words and icons
Low mist #8f9db4 Muted and disabled text, always with its reason in words
Moon (accent) #9cc3f5 Main button, mark of the current place, "In use"
Deep moon #6f9fdc Pressed main button
Veil 0.62 to 0.96 Strength of the veil under text; a brighter picture needs more
Frost #f4efe6 Main text, focus ring, the lit row and the ring of the lit cover
Amber #f0c26b Warns of something that changes the experience
Coral #ff9d8a An error or a destructive action
Sprout #8fd6a8 Done, healthy
Amber, Coral, Sprout deep #7a5200, #a8361f, #1d7040 The three on Frost (6.0, 5.7, 5.3:1)

The first eight change with the wallpaper (reiwand-palette derives them); Frost and the state tones never change.

The same names are used on every surface: Look.night, Tones.night on the phone, --night on the server. The server spells the accent --accent and --accent-deep, and carries the veil as --veil plus --night-rgb (phone: Tones.veiled(alpha)).

On the server the tones come from a generated stylesheet per cover (band/sheet.rs, css) and the class f-none (frame.rs, NO_WALLPAPER) is the fixed set. A veil or a shadow is always rgb(var(--night-rgb) / alpha); never a loose black on the server.

Rules#

The Own-Tones Rule. Every tone comes from the wallpaper in use and has a name. Do not add a colour to a page, a component or a Kotlin screen. If one is needed, it is a change to reiwand-palette. The phone and the server have no colours of their own.

The One-Lit-Thing Rule. One thing is lit per view: the focused cover (Frost ring, no dimming), the focused row (Frost ground, Night bold text) or the current tool. Everything else rests at Mist over Night. When two screens exist, the chosen one is marked, not lit.

The Scarce-Accent Rule. Moon appears in three places only: the mark of the current place, the main action and "In use". One main button per view (per state on the server). If the accent lands on a fourth thing, the first three lose their meaning.

The State-Tones Rule. Amber, Coral and Sprout are for state only, always with an icon and words beside them. They tint the icon, not large areas; the text stays Frost or Mist. Amber warns, Coral fails or destroys, Sprout confirms. On a lit row (Frost ground) use the deep variants: in QML ask Tone.of(tone, lit, rest) (qml/Tone.qml) instead of choosing the colour, and Tone.icon(tone) for the icon. In the server CSS the classes .good, .notice and .bad on .status, .status-line, .note and .field.bad do it.

The Coral-Confirm Rule. Only an irreversible action is confirmed, and its button is coral. What can be undone is not confirmed; it gets a notice with "Undo".

  • Desktop: the main Button of a deletion carries palette.highlight: Look.coral; SmallPanel { destructive: true } does it for a confirmation.
  • Phone: Kind.Remove in Buttons.kt (filled Coral, Night text, with its icon), the only main button of that sheet.
  • Server: .button.danger (coral text on High night), inside .confirm in the lit row. A coral fill would not read on Frost.
  • A destructive action that is only offered (it still leads to the confirmation) is coral words, never filled.

The Text-On-Deep Rule. Body text is at least 4.5:1. Text over a picture sits only inside a block with a veil: BandVeil and TitleVeils on the desktop, the title veil on the phone and .band::before on the server. The palette crate guarantees the ratios of the tones (palette.md); you guarantee that text is on Night or on a veil.

The No-Blur Rule. No blur, no backdrop-filter, no glass, no decorative gradient. Veils are gradients of Night with alpha, nothing else, and never over the whole picture on a title screen.

The Dim-Until-Lit Rule. Resting covers are dimmed (Look.coverVeil, 0.42); the lit cover loses the dimming and gains the ring and the big shadow.

Typography#

Red Hat Display (variable, Look.titleFont) carries menus, titles, buttons and the names of covers and rows: confident, tightly tracked (-0.02em to -0.03em at large sizes). Onest (Look.textFont) carries explanations, captions and values, with tabular figures. Both are self-hosted under the SIL OFL.

Desktop scale (Look.t*, pixels; compact / normal / roomy where it steps)

Wallpaper title tCover 52 / 66 / 80 bold. Screen title and empty state tPage 42 / 50 / 58 bold. Large heading tLarge 26 / 34 / 38. Menu and title of the lit cover tMenu, tFocus 22 / 27 / 30 (bold when current). Row label tRow 20 / 22 / 24. tSection 20, tAside 16 or 18, tFilter 18. All of these are Red Hat Display. In Onest: tExplain 17, tName 16, tBody 15 (running text), tFoot 13.5, tKey 12.5. The button is tButton 16.5, bold, Red Hat Display.

Look.tier steps by window height: short up to 680, compact up to 800, roomy from 1800 x 1000, else normal. The window sets it and every measure that depends on it follows. A page never reads the window size to pick a font size.

The editor's workbench is a tool surface and runs denser: while it is in view Look.desk is on and every type size follows (tBody, tName, tButton, tRow, tSection, tFilter 13, tFoot, tAside, tExplain 12, tTool 12.5, and the large ones capped at 13), with tool buttons 24 px, fields 26 px, controls and buttons 28 to 30 px and menu options 30 px. Its frame, tab strips, rows and blueprints take their measures from DeskLook (rows of 30 px, 13 px names, 12 px kinds).

Phone (Type in Type.kt, sp, follows the system text size)#

titleL 50, titleM 32, titleS 21, empty 26, row 17, button 16, word 16, fold 15.5, group 14, tab 14, text 15.5, foot 14, digits 44 (the pairing code). Leading is 1.45 for text and 1.3 for titles. Never go below 14 sp, and check the screen at the largest system text size.

Server (style.css, rem, fluid)#

--t-cover 3.5 (2.5 on phone widths), --t-section 2 (1.625), --t-row 1.3125 (1.1875), --t-large 1.1875, --t-text 1.0625, --t-foot 0.9375, --t-small 0.8125. Body line height is 1.55. Text is limited to --measure (68ch).

Rules#

The Line-Height Rule. Headings use text-wrap: balance and paragraphs pretty. Do not force <br>, and leave no lonely last word. Put a non-breaking space between a number and its unit (2&nbsp;GB).

The Words-Not-Numbers Rule. Give values in words ("Soft", "Medium", "Slow computer"), with the number inside the selector. Quality numbers, fingerprints, hashes, paths and variables never appear in the main view. They go behind "Details".

Layout and spacing#

Spacing is a scale and nothing else.

  • Desktop: Look.e1 to e8 = 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. margin 56, menuWidth 312, covers cover 256, coverFocus 420, coverGrid 232, shelfGap 28, always 16:9. rowHeight 74, rowsWidth 660 with an explainWidth column of 300. controlHeight 40, buttonHeight 42, toolHeight 32. The shelf aligns the bottoms of the covers; do not align them by hand.
  • Phone: Measure.e1 to e10 = 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40 dp. Margin 20, touch 48, bar 64, row 64, option 56, field 52. Text over the picture sits only in the bands the title veil covers; the brightest point of a picture never sits under text.
  • Server: --e-1 to --e-9 (0.25 to 6 rem). One column of --column, section gap --e-8 (--e-7 on phone widths), head --band 23 rem (17 on phone widths), control height 3 rem. Forms are 32 rem wide with the label above and the help and error below; the reason a button is disabled sits beside it (.why).

The Size-From-Tokens Rule. Use the tokens for tone, size and space: Look, Measure and Type, or the -- variables. A loose hex or pixel value in a page is a bug. If a value has no token and will be used twice, add the token. The only singletons beside Look are PreviewLook (measures of the switch-with-preview screen: reel of covers, badges, overlay timings) and PublishLook (the numbered steps and the width of the publish screen) and DeskLook (the frame of the editor's workbench); keep one-screen measures there.

Notices have one place per surface. They show one at a time: above the band on title screens, at the foot of the panel above the keys on options screens, top left of the work area on the work surface. Pages never position them (OptionsFrame.noticeAt, WorkFrame.noticeAt).

Depth, shapes and icons#

Depth is tonal layering: picture, veil, Night panel, High night element, Frost lit thing. Shadows are soft and dark, never coloured, never hard-offset.

  • Use the Shadow piece (qml/Shadow.qml) as a child of an opaque background. Its kind is cover (every cover), focus (the lit cover), row (the lit row) or panel (small panel, menu, notice). It exists only when the window draws with the graphics card, so the surface must look right without it. Photo rounds covers (corner, lowCorner).
  • On the server use --shadow (covers, notes, images) and --shadow-row (the lit row).

Shapes are quiet and rectangular. Look.radius and --radius (6 px) for covers, buttons, rows, panels, notices and fields; radiusSmall (4) for small icons, close buttons and thumbnails; keyRadius (5) for key caps; panelRadius (10) for small panels and menus. The phone adds rNotice (10 dp) and rSheet (22 dp). Round shapes are rare: the logo moon, radio buttons and the selection badge of a chosen cover.

A mark of place is a small bar (markWidth x markHeight, 16 x 4, 1 px radius) beside or under the current word; on the phone it sits on the top edge of the current tab. A focused thing gets a 2 px Frost outline with a 3 px gap (ring, ringGap), never a border. Lines are 1 px Night line.

Icons are Phosphor, in the reiwand icon theme: regular at rest, -fill when active or current, -bold in key hints. Every icon travels with a word, except "close" (give it an accessible name) and keys. A name the set lacks is not drawn, and the app tests fail on it (look.md, "Icons").

Motion is Look.pace (180 ms) with Look.paceCurve on colour, background, shadow and position. On the server transitions are off under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.

Components#

Use the piece, not a copy of its look. The properties named here are the real ones.

Buttons#

  • Main: Button { highlighted: true }. Moon ground, Night text, Red Hat Display bold, 42 px. Pressed goes Deep moon and drops 1 px. Only one per view, with its Enter key hint beside it on the desktop. Phone: Action (Kind.Main) pinned at the bottom of a sheet or page. Server: .button.primary.
  • Quiet: the default Button, High night with a 1 px Night line border, bold title font. Phone QuietAction; server .button.
  • Word: Button { flat: true }, words only, underline on hover. Phone WordAction; server .button.link.
  • Remove: see the Coral-Confirm Rule.
  • Disabled: always with its reason in words. Hardware that was not found stays visible, grey, with "not detected".
  • Focus: keyboard focus is the Frost ring of the style, or :focus-visible / .keyboard-focus on the server. Never draw an outline of your own. Inside a clipped container leave Look.ringRoom so the ring is not cut.

Menu words and filters#

MenuWord: Red Hat Display, Mist at rest, Frost and bold when current, with the accent mark on its left; Settings and Account sit apart. Filter: words with a small count, the current one with the mark under it. Phone Tabs: three tabs, the current one with the mark and a filled icon. Server .nav: the same words, with the mark under .current; "Sign out" is a form button.

Cover#

Cover (Cover.qml): 16:9 Photo, High night ground, dimmed at rest, name in Red Hat Display. lit: true makes it larger, undimmed, ringed in Frost, with Shadow { kind: "focus" }, and the title, caption and "Set as wallpaper" (the main button) below it. "In use" is the small accent label inside the cover (inUse). chosen shows a round check badge. There is never a banner, a featured cover, a rating or a popularity badge. Phone: Cover and Thumb in Cover.kt.

Rows#

ListRow (ListRow.qml) is one setting. Set kind for the value (words, switch, field, key capture, rail, arrows, a caret for a second level), explain for the column that opens beside the lit row, off for a disabled row, status and statusTone or note and noteTone for state. Resting: Night, Mist label. Hover: High night. Lit (lit, from activeFocus): Frost ground, Night bold text, deep state tones, Shadow { kind: "row" }. A disabled row is Low mist and still shows why. All rows share rowHeight and one midline; reiwand-app --check fails otherwise.

Phone: Setting, Group, Fold, Flip and Seam in Rows.kt. Server .rows > .row with .row-name, .row-value, .row-action: the name, the state in words (.status), a detail after a "·", and the button that fixes it beside it. .row.lit or :focus-within lights it and every child turns Night, except .button.danger (stays coral) and pre.fact (stays Mist).

Panels and small surfaces#

Opaque Night, 1 px line, Shadow { kind: "panel" }. SmallPanel has a cover header (cover), title, text, primary and quiet buttons and a foot. Menus and drop-downs use the style's Menu and ComboBox (38 px options, Night line, radius 10). On the phone use Sheet (handle, title, note, SheetOption with a mark when chosen) and Question for a question; there are no centred dialogs. On the server a confirmation is .confirm inside the lit row, with a line above.

Workbench#

The editor is a workbench of groups (SceneGroup): each holds panels as tabs on a strip, a panel moves by dragging its tab or from its menu, and the tabs that do not fit go to the strip's "⋯". Six factory layouts (Scene, Particles, Scripts, Simulation, Animation, Wallpaper; Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+6) hold only the panels of their kind of work, four to seven, and keep the rest closed: Window opens any of them where it usually sits (package pages that are not scenes start with every panel they have). The person's changes are kept per layout. Each layout is drawn as its blueprint (SceneBlueprint, from the same tree that builds it) beside its name in the top bar, in Window and in the list of layouts, and the zone a dragged tab will take is painted in the same language: no other picture names a layout. Below DeskLook.folds the left side folds into a rail of icons whose panel unfolds over the canvas; F11 enlarges the panel in focus and a panel can go to a window of its own.

  • Tones: while the editor is in view (a scene's or another package's) its tones come from the thumbnail of the package being edited, the app's own set when it has none, with the same names (SceneEditorTones, Look.tone); leaving it gives back the tones the app had. Light editor is an editor-only preference (EditorLight, editor.conf, [look] light), in the editor's View menu and in Settings › Wallpapers › Making wallpapers: the same hues as paper surfaces and ink text (reiwand_palette::paper, Look.light), every text tone at 4.5:1 or more on every surface; lit rows become ink on paper and the focus ring takes the text tone. The rest of the app stays dark. Build editor pieces from the tone names only, so they read in both sets; SceneTonesCheck turns the light set on and off.

  • Value rows (SceneInput, SceneValueRow): the name on the left, the editor beside it, the reset and keyframe buttons after it. On the workbench the names make one column (DeskLook.label, less the indent of a group), so editors start on one line; a row too narrow for it takes room from the name, never from the editor's floor.

  • Language rows (Texts and languages): the language is a column of its own and sits, like the remove button, on the first line of the field, whatever its height; a field keeps its width when the remove button is hidden.

  • Buttons in a row keep their place when they hide (opacity, not visible), so the eyes, locks and solos of the layers stay in columns.

  • Nothing sets the panel's width: a row never asks for more than a narrow panel has (wrap the words, fold a button to its icon) and nothing reads a width it sets itself, which loops on a fractional scale. Content keeps clear of an overlaid scroll bar (ScrollColumn).

  • Type: the window's title (15 px) is the largest; a panel's tab is 13 px and nothing inside a panel is larger: section heads 13 px semibold (PanelHeading), names, values and body 13 px, secondary words 12 px. Controls with no size of their own follow it (the window's font while the workbench is in view); SceneTypeCheck fails on larger words in any panel of any layout.

  • Rows of names and controls in a panel use SceneGrid (two columns) or SceneRowFit (a row): their names wrap and their check boxes and drop-down lists narrow, down to a floor, before a control is pushed past the panel's edge.

  • Bars give up words in turn when short of room: the top bar's Undo and Redo, then the layout names, then the words of its buttons; the canvas bar's Test the pointer and Capture.

  • Focus: the item chosen is lit only in the group that has the keyboard, or had it last (SceneFocus.group); the other groups keep it marked in High night with a line (marked of the style's ItemDelegate, lit through SceneFocus.lit). The accent under the current tab is drawn only on that group. F6 and Shift+F6 go from group to group, Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab from tab to tab; on an edge the arrows move it 4 px, 40 px with Shift.

Check each panel at 1600 x 900 and 1366 x 768, at scale 1 and 1.25 (1280 and 1093 wide), in every layout and package page.

Notices, state, empty and error#

  • Toast (Toast.qml): High night ground with a line, an icon in the state tone (tone), one line, an optional action, a close button. One visible at a time, never blocking. Phone: NoticeAsk in Notice.kt. Server .note with .good, .notice, .bad.
  • StatusLine: icon plus words (tone, lit). Server .status and .status-line.
  • Note, SmallNote: a quiet line under a row or a panel.
  • Empty: one big sentence and the first step as an action. Server .empty.
  • Failure and Details: what happened, what to do, one button that fixes it, the technical part behind "Details". Server .more (.more.fix for a fixing step).
  • Progress: words, a rail, and "Stop" when it can be stopped.

Keys and fields#

KeyCap and Hints (pairs of keys and words) sit at the foot of the band or the panel. Only arrows, Enter, Esc and Tab; at most two hints on a title screen; Enter appears once, beside the main button.

Desktop fields are the style's controls (look.md, "Controls"); a test fails if a screen uses a control the style does not draw. Server forms: form.form, .field (label above, .help, .field-error, .field.bad), .checkbox, .choices > .choice, .file (the native input is never in view; a button plus .file-name), .fact, .pairs and .copy (a datum that selects whole with one click, and a "Copy" button). The only script the server serves is assets/copy.js; the page works without it.

Do#

  • Take every tone from the wallpaper in use, through Look, Tones or the CSS variables.
  • Light one thing per view; give the accent to the current place, the main action and "In use".
  • Keep one main button per view, and a disabled control with its reason in words beside it.
  • Write state in words with an icon and its tone; use Tone.of on lit rows.
  • Confirm only what cannot be undone, with the coral button; offer "Undo" for the rest.
  • Say what happened, put the button that fixes it beside the message and fold the technical part behind "Details".
  • Keep touch targets at 48 dp and text at 14 sp or more on the phone.
  • Use the user's vocabulary: "Set as wallpaper", "In use", "Slow computer / Fast computer". "Repository" stays "repository"; a source is a place that gives pictures. "Author key" is the secret that signs and is never just "key"; "signing" is the act and its result. The server also speaks of People (who enters, invitations, accounts) and Review (what people upload); Administration is only the health of the server.

Don't#

  • Do not make it look like a store: no banners, featured covers, ratings, prices, download counts or autoplay carousels. The shelf is the person's own covers.
  • Do not make it look like a technical panel: no ids, paths, fingerprints, hashes or REIWAND_* names in the main view.
  • Do not wrap everything in a bordered card, or use loose colours or sizes in a page.
  • Do not put an icon alone (except "close" and keys), or draw your own focus outline.
  • Do not use keyboard hints or hover on the phone, or add authoring tools to it.
  • Do not add an eyebrow or small-caps label above titles.

Checking#

Run target/debug/reiwand-app --check (it loads every QML file and checks row heights and focus rings). Look at the app with a bright wallpaper, a dark one and the "no wallpaper" set; test the phone at the largest system text size; load server pages with and without a cover, at phone width and with the keyboard only.
