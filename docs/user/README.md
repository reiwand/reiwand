# Getting started

Reiwand puts animated wallpapers on your desktop and uses as little as possible: if nothing
changes on the screen, it does no work. This page takes you from opening the app to having a
wallpaper set.

## What you see when you open the app

- The wallpaper you have set fills the whole window.
- On the left there is a menu of words: Library, Explore, Lists, Sources and Create. Further down
  are Settings and Account. The word of the place you are in is shown in bold.
- At the top right it says what the current wallpaper is, with its controls.
- At the bottom right, a line shows the keys that work right now.

The first time, if you have no wallpapers, the Library says "You have no wallpapers yet". The app
installs one of the included examples by itself and sets it as the wallpaper; a notice says so and
offers "Undo".

## Choosing a wallpaper

1. Go to Library. The covers are at the bottom; use the ← and → arrows to move through them.
2. Press Enter, or the "Set as wallpaper" button. It is set at once.
3. With the pointer resting on the cover of a scene you see it move, without changing your desktop.

The filters at the top (All, Scenes, Images, Videos, Web, From sources and Favorites) and the
search box help when you have many.

## One wallpaper per screen

With several screens, "Set as wallpaper" becomes "Set as wallpaper on …" and sets it on the
chosen screen. At the top right, a strip shows each screen; choosing one changes which one you are
talking to. Screens are called "Laptop" or "Monitor". In Settings › Screens, "Layouts" saves which
wallpaper goes on each screen and puts them back when you connect or remove one.

## Lists that change by themselves

A list is a group of wallpapers that take turns. In Lists, "New playlist…" creates one and in
"Changes every" you choose how often it changes. To build one in one go, choose several wallpapers
in the Library and use "Rotate these every…". "Start" turns it on.

## Pausing

At the top right, the pause button stops the wallpaper and resumes it. If the screen is rotating a
list, "Pause the list" stops only the changing. In Settings › Screens, the "Pause" row decides
whether the wallpaper stops by itself when a full-screen window covers it, on battery or with the
screen locked.

## The tray icon

On Plasma, the tray icon lets you pause, move to the next wallpaper, raise or lower the master
volume, mute and open the app without going into it. See "The tray" in
[the app guide](app.md).

## Where the settings are

In Settings, with three tabs: Wallpapers, Screens and Device. The search box at the top finds a
row by its name and takes you to it. Esc goes back one step.

## Getting more wallpapers

- Explore shows the wallpapers of the repositories you have. Installing them is one click.
- "Add a repository" (in Explore and in "Repositories…") adds one by pasting its address.
- Sources brings photos from the internet (Bing, NASA, Wikimedia and others) and rotates them by
  itself.
- "Add wallpapers…" in the Library adds your own files; you can also drop them on the window.

With Private mode (Settings › Device) the whole network is cut and only what is already on your
computer remains.
