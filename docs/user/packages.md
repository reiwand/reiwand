# Packages: rating, languages and credits

A package is a wallpaper, a preset or a set of parts that Reiwand installs and plays. Its manifest
(`docs/specs/package.md`) declares its rating, languages, credits, inputs and what it needs from
the engine. These pages explain what packages declare, how the app treats them, and what scenes can
do on the desktop and the phone.

- [The package manifest](packages/manifest.md): what a manifest declares, languages, formatted text and kinds of package.
- [Mature content, flashing and search](packages/ratings-and-search.md): blurred thumbnails, photosensitive confirmation and search facets.
- [Assets, presets and dependencies](packages/assets-and-presets.md): assets packages, separately published presets and required packages.
- [Accent color, playback and shortcuts](packages/playback.md): accent color, frame rate, volume, quality levels and shortcuts.
- [Scene features](packages/scene-features.md): what scenes describe, and the camera, text, video, blend modes and layer features already drawn.
- [Timelines, sound and video clocks](packages/timelines-and-sound.md): timeline events, sound layers, video clocks and HDR video.
- [Package effects](packages/package-effects.md): WGSL effects of the package, their checks, crash protection and cost.
- [Built-in effects](packages/built-in-effects.md): new built-in effects and new parameters of existing ones.
- [Advanced scenes](packages/advanced-scenes.md): overview of meshes, models, particles, declared inputs and pointer on solid layers.
- [Skeleton meshes](packages/meshes.md): mesh layers, clips, inverse kinematics, bone physics, depth and scripts.
- [Particles](packages/particles.md): emission, initializers, operators, flocks, collisions, trails, ropes and script control.
- [Lights](packages/lights.md): light layers, lit layers, volumetric light, projected textures and lit particles.
- [3D models](packages/models.md): glTF models, 3D groups, materials, camera, animation and shadows.
- [Compatibility and versions](packages/compatibility.md): engine features, older Reiwand versions and reference tables.
