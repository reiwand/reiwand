# The desktop app

The app, screen by screen, in the order of the menu on the left. The names are the ones you see in
the app. To start from scratch, read the [getting started](README.md) page first.

- [Keys and the library](app/library.md): the keys that work everywhere, and the Library screen
  with the options of a wallpaper.
- [Getting and making wallpapers](app/getting-wallpapers.md): Explore, Sources, Accounts and
  Create.
- [Lists, the preview and the tray](app/lists-and-tray.md): lists that take turns, changing the
  wallpaper with a preview, and the Plasma tray.
- [Settings and permissions](app/settings.md): the Settings tabs, moving your settings,
  permission questions and private mode.
