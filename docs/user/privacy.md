# Privacy

What the program does with the network and with your data. There is no telemetry, nothing connects
unless you add it, and no scene sees your sound, pointer or clicks without your permission.

- [Network and updates](privacy/network.md): private mode, checks for a new version, providers,
  repositories, web wallpapers, RGB lighting, models and servers.
- [Stored data and secrets](privacy/stored-data.md): where keys, passwords, the configuration and
  the library are kept, and with which permissions.
- [Scenes and what they can see](privacy/scene-permissions.md): permissions for sound, pointer and
  clicks, the media player and scene scripts.
