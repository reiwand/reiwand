On this page: the stream, the preview level, testing the pointer, comparing and the glow.

The preview stream#

The app asks the daemon for a virtual output named preview:<n> with RequestStream and shows its PipeWire stream on the canvas, as the Plasma wallpaper does. The stream has the pixels the canvas takes on the screen (its size times the screen's pixel ratio, rounded up to 640-pixel steps so that zooming or resizing the window asks for a new stream only now and then), never more than the scene's own size; at 100 % zoom and zoomed in each screen pixel has a pixel of its own. The levels of Preview quality that draw smaller say it in their row (Q0 half size, Q1 60 to 75 %); Q2 and Q3 draw the full size of the stream. Every change sends the unsaved document with PreviewScene (dbus.md), at most once per 50 ms, with solo applied; a paused or scrubbed timeline holds the scene at that second, so a still preview renders only when something changes. A new document replaces the preview without a crossfade, and a playing preview keeps its time; package details that are still incomplete, such as a missing rating, do not stop it. View as wallpaper shows the same preview on the desktop, on the chosen screen or on all of them. The app repeats the call every 30 seconds while a preview is on and ends it when the editor closes; the daemon ends it 60 seconds after the last call. A minimized window marks the preview output covered. reiwand-ctl scene preview DIR [-o OUTPUT] [--time T] [--quality Q] [--restart] shows the saved folder for 60 seconds, at most at that quality level and started again, and --stop ends it; --media FILE shows a made-up track from a JSON file of at most 64 KiB (title, artist, album, album_artist, subtitle, genres, the last three words or lists of words, playing, position and duration in seconds from 0 to 10 000 000 and rate from −100 to 100, cover, a picture inside the folder; texts of at most 512 characters without control characters) instead of a media player. The document of a preview may carry preview: { quality, restart, media, battery, calm }, which the daemon reads only for previews; media is refused, naming the field, when a value is out of range, and never reads the media players or needs the media grant. battery and calm are booleans: the preview then runs as the daemon runs a scene on battery (a lower level, which takes Q3 to Q2 whatever the automatic choice would be, and at most 60 frames a second) or with reduced motion (parallax and camera shake stand still, as the daemon does with the desktop setting). reiwand-ctl scene preview DIR [--battery] [--calm] [--without-bloom] sets them (--without-bloom shows the preview without the glow of the canvas, to compare it with the glow).

A preview document that carries scene.ext.editor.probe ({pointer: [x, y], tilt: [x, y]}, the pointer as fractions of the canvas from 0 to 1 and the tilt of a phone from −1 to 1, either part optional) feeds the scene a pointer that rests there and that tilt, so layers with parallax depth sit in the picture where the canvas outlines put them; out-of-range values are clamped, and the daemon reads it only for previews. The scene runtime takes the tilt as a device tilt, so canvas.parallax.gyro follows it.

Preview level#

Preview level, a button of the canvas toolbar, opens the panel that sets what the preview shows. It has the same quality selector as the cost panels (Q0, Q1, Q2, Q3 and Restart), On battery and Reduced motion, and every change sends the preview again. Under them the panel answers the editor query {"level": {"quality", "battery", "calm"}} (rigs::query, levels::query): quality is the level the daemon reaches (asked is the cap that was chosen), render_scale, density and particles are what that level keeps of the resolution, the crowd and the particles, bloom says whether the canvas has a glow (on), whether it has a glare above white (hdr) and whether this level draws it (glare: Q3, or Q2 off battery), effects has one row per effect use with its layer, effect, whether it is kept, its scale, its units of work at this level (null when it is left out), the full_units it costs at Q3, the levels of all four levels as the effect-cost panel shows them and follows when it reacts to the pointer, pointer.emitters are the particle systems that follow the pointer, left_out counts the effects left out and units adds up the work that stays. The panel writes the same rows, in bold when an effect is left out, and names what follows the pointer so that it can be tried with Test the pointer and clicks. The cost panel of an effect marks the level the daemon would reach, not the one that was asked for.

Testing the pointer, comparing and the glow#

While Test the pointer and clicks is on, the preview shows a ring where the pointer is, red while a button is held, and every press sends out a pulse from the point it fell on, so that the author sees where a click reached the scene.

Compare with picks a second level and Without the glow of the canvas the same frame with the bloom taken out (look.bloom: false leaves canvas.bloom out of the previewed document, never out of the saved one). Holding Hold to see the other side shows that second look in the same preview and letting go returns to the first, so two levels, or the glow on and off, can be told apart on the same frame; Split the picture with the other side in the panel writes both looks into one picture, the first on the left and the second on the right.

Compare the glow, a button of the canvas toolbar that appears while the canvas has bloom, splits the canvas itself in two live views: the app asks the daemon for a second stream of the preview (RequestStream, released when the button is turned off, the canvas changes size or the editor closes) and sends it the same document without canvas.bloom (SceneEditor.halve, the property split carries { on, node, serial }). The left of the canvas shows the first stream, with the glow, and the right the second, without it, cut by a line with a handle that is dragged to move the split (the position is not saved). Both streams follow the same time and edits, the simulated pointer and clicks reach both, and both are covered together. reiwand-ctl scene preview DIR --without-bloom previews without the glow from the terminal.
