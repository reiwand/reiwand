The Add layer menu offers 3D model (once the scene has a .glb asset), 3D group and 3D model from a file, a dialog that reads a binary glTF file before it is imported and tells its triangles, materials, textures, bones and clips, and for each device tier whether it fits, the largest texture it gets (2048 px on phones and on T0 and T1) and the texture memory, with a note on converting FBX and OBJ with Blender; accepting imports the file and adds a model layer, inside the selected group.

A scene with models draws over the preview an authoring view with its own camera, kept in ext.editor.view3d, which only the preview reads: dragging with the right button turns it around its target, with the middle button or Shift slides it and the wheel brings it closer, and the preview renders through it while the scene camera shows as a dashed frustum with a handle on its eye, a square on the point it looks at, both moved in the plane of the view, and a line toward its up. Each model shows its box, which selects it with a click; the selected model shows three arrows along x, y and z that move it along that direction (the point of the axis closest to the pointer, so the model stays under it whatever the angle of the view), three rings around x, y and z with a knob each that turns it around that axis of the scene, a Depth square that moves it along the line of sight (up pushes it away, down brings it closer, the model staying under the pointer), a knob above it that turns it (sideways around y, up and down around x) and a square on its box corner that scales it. These handles start from the values the model has at the current time and write position, rotation and scale: a keyed value gets a key at the current time, a value bound to a property or a script keeps its binding and its handle is dimmed (a drag with Shift replaces the binding with the value), and scene3d model set refuses to write over keys or a binding. The arrows, rings, Depth and the handles of the camera, of camera path keys and of lights all send scene3d grab (what: model with layer, eye, center, key with path, key (its time) and aim, or light with layer and aim; handle: x, y, z, turn_x, turn_y, turn_z, plane, depth, orbit, dolly or roll; from and to in pixels of the canvas as stage3d reports them; time, the playhead, where a keyed value gets its key; replace, which writes over a key or a binding). The editor works out the move with the camera of the renderer: the ray through each pixel, the axis or plane it meets and the angle around the axis, so the result lands where the renderer then draws it.

The inspector of a model layer shows the counts and the tier verdicts of its file, a Reflects picker of the pictures of the scene (env) with a thumbnail, Look at it in the authoring view, and a Materials part with a row per material of the file: a ball shaded with the material and the light around and from above of the scene (as in the light tools), the shader picker (physical, unlit or a package effect), lit, both sides, casts and takes shadows, tint and glow colors, sliders for metal, roughness, relief and occlusion, and the thumbnails of the maps of the file (color, metal and roughness, normals, occlusion, glow). The inspector of a group turns it 3D, and the inspector of a flat layer inside a 3D group toggles Hide behind nearer models (depth_test); on a particles layer a note under it says, from the depth3d query, whether each particle hides on its own (and that the lowest quality hides the whole layer as one plane) or why the whole layer hides as one plane: its system has no perspective; for refracting particles it adds that their bending hides too but can still pull in a few pixels of a nearer model next to it (caveat: refract_edges).

Selecting the canvas also shows the 3D camera: sliders for the field of view and the roll that change the preview while they move, where it looks from and toward, Set the camera from the view, Look through the camera and Back to the camera view. In the authoring view the scene camera shows a knob on its eye and a square on the point it looks at, which move in the plane of the view or, with Ctrl, along the line of sight; a ring around its line of sight with a Roll knob that turns its up; an Orbit knob that swings the eye around the point it looks at, keeping the distance; and a Dolly square that brings the eye closer (up) or backs it away (down) along the line of sight; and Bright colors: a switch for hdr, the tone map picker and a Before and after slider that keeps ext.editor.tonemap_split, so the preview shows the part on the left without the tone map. The quality picker of the lights panel lists for each level the triangles, the 3D groups, the work units of the models and whether reflections of pictures are drawn, and for each tier the quality it starts at, whether the models fit and their texture memory. reiwand-ctl scene act DIR scene3d, group3d, material, camera3d, view3d, tonemap, model-info and cost3d do the same from the terminal, and group3d … show prints the handles the preview draws.

The inspector of a model with a skeleton or clips adds an Animation part: its clips with their length and animation, On the timeline (the clip timeline of meshes, whose rows for a model key angles and shift of the chosen bone and the weights of its blend shapes, and which shows the events of the clip as marks that seek to them), New clip from the file with the animations of the file and their length, Root motion, Swinging bones (a bone picker and the presets, with the bodies the layer has), a slider per blend shape for its weight, and Animation layers: per layer the clip it plays, On, Adds, move up, remove, sliders for weight, speed, fade in and fade out, and a bone mask picker (Only these bones); the same Animation layers part shows under the bones of a mesh. In the authoring view the selected model draws its bones (a line from each bone to its parent and a knob that picks the bone for the timeline and the pickers) and, with root motion, the path the layer will walk ahead from where it stands, mixing every playing animation layer by its weight (adding the additive ones), over two turns of its longest clip and in all three directions, turning where the clips turn. The picked bone shows a ring: dragging the ring turns the bone around the view axis, dragging inside it tips the bone like a trackball, and a square in its middle moves it in the plane of the view; each drag keys angles or shift of that bone in the chosen clip at the playhead (a model without clips gets clip_1). A swinging bone draws the fan its rotation bound allows in its own plane, with Least and Most knobs that drag the bound, and under Swinging bones each body shows its wind slider, the bounds of its turn and slide and Show the wind on the preview, which draws the arrow of the wind of the scene. A body with a translation bound draws the box its head may slide in, along x, y and z of the model around the head it is pinned to, and the picked bone gets Least and Most knobs on its x edge that drag the bound. Colliders lists the colliders of the model: Add a collider on the picked bone, a capsule along the bone or a sphere at its head, a radius slider and Remove; the view draws each one as dashed circles, with a knob on the rim that drags the radius. Chains that reach lists the IK chains of the model with New chain from the parent of the picked bone to it, the target (a point, the pointer, a bone of the model or a layer), the weight and Remove; a point target shows a knob in the view that drags it, in pixels of the box of the layer. rigs::query answers bone3d (layer, bone, clip, time, clip_time, from with the values the drag started at, and turn in degrees, tilt [x, y] in degrees or slide [x, y] in pixels) with the angles and shift to key, and bound3d (layer, bone, point) with the degrees of the bound under a point, and surface3d (at a point of the canvas in pixels, time) with the model surface under it in the authoring view (layer, material, uv, point, normal, unit: the size of a pixel of the canvas there, and color of its surface), or null; the cast follows the skinned pose of the time but not the blend shapes; reiwand-ctl scene act DIR key and scene act DIR physics do the same from the terminal.

A material row picks Fur, Foliage or Chroma key besides Physical and Unlit and the material shaders of the package: fur shows sliders for shells, length and density, the shells each quality level draws, a comb pad for the comb of the whole material (dragged across for x and y, a slider toward the viewer) and Comb on the model, a brush of the authoring view: while it is on, a drag over the model draws the stroke with a ring of the brush size and, when released, combs the fur where it passed (comb, one step of undo per stroke), with sliders for the brush size and the strength, Erase, and Clear the combing; foliage shows sway and speed, the wind of the canvas with its arrow; chroma shows a key color picker, Pick the key color from the model (a click on the model in the authoring view sets the key, key), and tolerance and feather sliders. Every material row has a Debug view: none, the sway mask of the vertex colors, the matte (its alpha after the key) or where shadows fall (lit white, shadowed red, unlit materials white); only that material shows it and the others are drawn as they are. Debug views live in ext.editor.debug3d, which only the author preview reads: a shown or packed wallpaper never draws them. A shader of the package shows a warning that it runs on the graphics card within the limits of package effects. Under the materials, New material shader writes a template, and every material shader of the package shows whether it builds, or its error with the line and column of its file. Edit the WGSL code… opens the shader in the code view of package shaders, with its live checks, its marked failing line and its undoable save (shader write), and Open in your editor… in that view hands its file to the editor of the desktop.

The lights panel shows Lights in 3D: per light a switch that places it in 3D where it shows now or sends it back to the plane (scene3d light). In the authoring view every light shows a knob where it lights the models (dimmed while it stays on the plane: dragging it places it in 3D) and, but for point lights, a square on the point it shines at with a dashed line between them; both move in the plane of the view or, with Ctrl, along the line of sight, keying position3d and aim3d at the playhead when they are keyed (grab with what light). stage3d lists each light with at, aim, space and whether its fields are keyed or bound. The lights panel shows Shadows: per point, spot or directional light a switch, the size of its map (automatic or 256 to 4 096) with the size each tier draws, on phones and at Q0, and a bias slider, and Show where shadows fall, which draws lit places white and shadowed ones red in the preview and the dashed frusta of the maps in the authoring view. The 3D camera panel shows Camera paths: per path its length, Loop, the easing, remove, its keys (a click removes one) and Key the view at the current time, Add from the view, the order, the fade with its length, and Fly through the paths from the camera, which looks through the scene camera and plays from the start. In the authoring view each path draws its curve, a knob on each key that moves the eye of that key and a square on the point it looks at, both moved in the plane of the view at their own depth or, with Ctrl, along the line of sight (grab with what key), a dashed line between them, and a square where the paths put the camera at the current time. The scene timeline shows a lane per path with its keys, which drag to another time (key with from, camera-path … key PATH T --from T in the terminal) and seek the preview with a click; the panel of the timelines takes the presses that no lane takes, because the Breeze style turns a drag that nothing accepts into a move of the whole window, which kept the keys of a lane from being dragged. The cost lines add the shadow maps, the fur shells, the fur triangles each shadow map draws (fur_shadow_triangles; left_out says when fur casts its skin only) and the skinned vertices of each quality level and the shadowed lights of each tier. reiwand-ctl scene animate, clip, key, material, shadows, camera-path, new-material, debug3d and cost3d do the same from the terminal.
