# Effects and layer fields

| Action | Arguments | Command |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| `effects` | one of `add` (`layer`, `effect`: a built-in effect or one of the package, optional `name`, `at` (the top when omitted), `params` and `optional`; starts the stack of a layer that has none), `set` (`layer`, `index`, optional `params`, where `null` goes back to the default, `enabled`, `name`, an empty one removes it, and `optional`), `remove` (`layer`, `index`; the last one removes the stack), `move` (`layer`, `index`, `to`), `grip` (`layer`, `index`, `param`, `to`, optional `both`, `at` and `replace`: what a handle of the canvas does when it is dragged to `to`, a point of the canvas in pixels as the preview draws it, under the parents, groups, anchor, origin and parallax of the layer; a `position` or point parameter takes the fraction of the layer under `to`, a direction takes the vector from the first point of the effect (the middle of the layer when it has none) to `to` over a fifth of the shorter side of the layer, each part from −1 to 1, and an angle takes the angle of that same vector in degrees, clockwise from the right, turned by whole circles to the one nearest to the current value within the range of the parameter; with `both`, the other end of a pair of points (`from` and `to`, `start` and `end`, `a` and `b`, `first` and `second`) moves by the same amount; refused for a parameter without a handle, a layer with no size when the parameter is a direction or an angle and a parameter that follows keyframes or another source unless `replace`) `mask_grip` (see Inspector) or `mask` (`layer`, optional `index`, the mask of the layer itself when it is left out, optional `texture`, `from` (a layer) or `shape`, one of them, which replaces the source of the mask, `channel`, `invert`, `feather`, `strength`, or `off`); refuses an effect it does not know, a parameter the effect lacks, a number outside the range of the parameter, a sound or light layer, a mask without a source and any problem the change adds to the scene, as the inspector does | `effects DIR LAYER add EFFECT [--param K=V] [--name N] [--at I] [--optional]`, `set I [--param K=V] [--unset K] [--enabled B] [--name N] [--optional B]`, `remove I`, `move I TO`, `grip` and `mask_grip` through `scene act DIR effects JSON`, `mask I [--texture A] [--from L] [--shape JSON] [--channel C] [--invert B] [--feather PX] [--strength S] [--off]` |
| `layer` | one of `transform` (`layer`, optional `position`, `rotation` (−360 to 360), `scale`, `skew`, `origin`, `tilt` (each angle −89 to 89), `z` (−100000 to 100000) and `size`, a box of an image, video, solid, adjustment or spectrum layer above 0; what is not given stays, and a given field that follows keyframes or another source is refused unless `replace` writes the plain value over it), `grip` (`layer`, `grip`, `to`, optional `snap`, `at` and `replace`: what a handle of the canvas does when it is dragged to `to`, a point of the canvas in pixels as the preview draws it, under the parents, groups, anchor, origin, parallax and 2D camera of the layer; `move` puts the pivot of the layer (its `position`) at `to`, and with `snap` (pixels) first pulls the edges and the middle of the layer to the edges and the middle of the canvas and of the other layers that are not inside it or follow it, when they are that close; `nudge` moves the pivot by `to`; `turn` points the top of the layer at `to`; `origin` moves the pivot to `to` and changes `origin` so that the layer stays where it is; on a tilted or lifted layer, or under a tilted parent, `to` is first brought back onto the layer as the canvas section below tells; `{ "tilt": { "axis" } }` and `lift` set `tilt` and `z` from the depth gizmo; `{ "scale": { "corner", "even" } }` scales the layer so that the point `corner` of its box (fractions, `[1, 1]` the bottom right, `[1, 0.5]` the middle of the right edge, which scales one side) lands on `to` while the pivot stays, keeping the proportions with `even`; `{ "skew": { "edge" } }` skews along the edge whose middle `edge` names, so that it passes through `to`; `{ "size": { "corner" } }` is for an adjustment layer that captures a region (it has `size`): it writes `size`, not the scale, so that the point `corner` of the captured rectangle (the middle of an edge changes one side) lands on `to` while the pivot stays, from 1 pixel up to twice the canvas on each side, in the coordinates of the parents and without the turn, skew or scale of the layer, as the capture is placed; the other grips each write as `transform` does, with `at` the second of the playhead), `fit` (`layer`, `fit`: `cover`, `contain` or `stretch`, and `at` and `replace`: scales and places the layer so that its box covers the canvas, fits inside it or fills it, keeping its rotation and skew, under its parents), `parent` (`layer`, optional `parent`, none to stop following, `keep`, and `at` and `replace`: sets or removes `parent`, refused for the layer itself, a sound or light layer, a layer that follows it already, one inside it, or a chain that would pass 8 layers; with `keep` it writes the position, rotation, skew and scale that leave the layer where it was), `blend` (`layer`, `blend`: a blend mode), `touch` (`layer`, `takes`: `off` lets the pointer through, `all` takes it over the whole box and `alpha` only where the layer is at least `alpha` opaque, 0 to 1, 0.5 or the one it had when omitted; writes or removes `solid`), `figure` (one of `shape`, `stop`, `text_width` or `fonts`, see "Shapes, text and fonts"), `depth` (`layer`, `depth`: `[across, up and down]`, each −10 to 10, the parallax depth of the layer, written as one number when both are the same and removed at 0, refused while the parallax follows keyframes or another source unless `replace` writes the plain value over it; it adds `pointer` to the inputs of the manifest when they lack it) or `instance` (`layer`, a particles layer, `values`: `alpha`, `size`, `count`, `speed`, `life`, `time_scale`, `brightness`, `color` or `points`, `null` drawing that value as the system does again, `points` as a list from `p0` or as an object of point numbers from 0 to 7 that changes only those points, `null` giving one back to the system, and `clear`); refuses what the scene would not take | `layer DIR LAYER grip move\|nudge\|turn\|origin\|scale\|skew X,Y [--corner U,V] [--even] [--snap PX] [--at S] [--replace]`, `fit cover\|contain\|stretch`, `parent [PARENT] [--keep]`, `transform [--position X,Y] [--rotation D] [--scale X,Y] [--skew X,Y] [--origin X,Y] [--tilt X,Y] [--z Z] [--size W,H] [--replace]`, `blend MODE`, `touch off|all|alpha [--alpha A]`, `instance [--alpha A] [--size S] [--count C] [--speed S] [--life L] [--time-scale T] [--brightness B] [--color C] [--point X,Y]... [--move-point K=X,Y]... [--unset-point K]... [--unset V] [--clear]` |
