animate one of: layer (layer: a mesh or a model; add (a clip: a new animation layer playing it) or index (0 first), then optional clip, weight 0 to 1, rate 0 to 16, additive, blend_in and blend_out 0 to 10 s, enabled, bones (the bones it moves; empty for all), to (a new place), remove; at most 16); root (layer: a model with clips, on: sets root_motion); preset (layer, bone, preset: hair, tail, cloth, antenna, chain or jelly, which set mode, stiffness, damping, inertia, gravity and wind of its body, adding one when it has none); morph (layer, shape of the file or an expression, weight −1 to 1 or null to drop it) animate DIR LAYER layer [--add CLIP | --index N] [--clip C] [--weight W] [--rate R] [--additive B] [--blend-in S] [--blend-out S] [--enabled B] [--bones A,B] [--to N] [--remove], animate DIR LAYER root true|false, animate DIR LAYER preset BONE PRESET, animate DIR LAYER morph SHAPE WEIGHT|none; animate DIR LAYER clips and bones print the clips of the layer and of the file with their events and root path, its animation layers, bones and blend shapes
scene3d one of: model with add (asset, optional id, into, at: a position), set (layer, optional position, rotation, scale: three numbers each, the scale never 0, size, env: a picture, empty removes it, asset) or remove (layer); group (optional id, into, layers to gather; makes a group with space: 3d); space (layer, deep); depth_test (layer, on; only flat layers inside a 3D group); material (layer, name of a material of the file, optional shader: pbr, unlit or a package effect, lit, double_sided, cast_shadows, receive_shadows, color, emissive (null removes them), params with metallic, roughness and occlusion 0 to 1 and normal_scale 0 to 10, values with any parameter of a package material shader, checked against its descriptor (a picture asset of the package or white, black, noise or normal_flat for its images, null removes one), maps (per slot base, orm, normal, occlusion or emissive, a picture asset of the scene, null gives back the map of the file), files (per slot, a picture file imported as a new asset, as data for orm, normal and occlusion, and chosen in the same step), clear; each map of the materials panel is a button with its thumbnail that opens a picker: the map of the file, a picture from a file or any picture of the scene with its thumbnail, and it warns when a data slot holds a picture read as colors); paint (layer, name of a material, slot, color as three numbers 0 to 1, radius as a share of the picture, optional hardness, points from 0 to 1 over the picture, 1 to 4096 of them) paints a stroke into that map, starting from the picture the scene chose, else the map of the file, else a white (or flat normal) picture of 512 px, and saves it under maps/ as the picture of the slot (as data for orm, normal and occlusion); Paint this map… in the picker of a map opens it flat with a brush, a color, a size and a hardness; camera (optional fov 1 to 179, near, far, eye, center, up, roll −180 to 180 degrees around the view, from_view, clear); view (the authoring view in ext.editor.view3d: orbit in degrees, pan in pixels, zoom as a factor, frame (a model layer), through, reset); tones (optional hdr, tonemap: none, aces, agx or srgb_to_linear, only with hdr, split 0 to 1 for the before and after preview); shadows (layer: a point, spot or directional light, optional on, resolution 256 to 4096, automatic (drops it), bias 0 to 1; at most 2 lights); path with add (id: a path of two keys, at 0 and 2 s, where the authoring view looks), key (path, time, optional eye, center, up, fov 1 to 179, from_view, from (moves that key), remove; a path keeps 2 keys), remove (path), set (path, optional loop, ease, to: a new id), order (sequential or random) or fade (optional to: black or cross, duration 0 to 10, clear); grab (a drag of a handle of the 3D view: what, handle, from, to, time, replace, as the model handles below describe); light (layer: a light, optional place (writes position3d, and aim3d except for a point light, where the light shows now), position, aim (three numbers within a million), flat (removes both, back to the plane)); key (layer, name of a chroma material, at: a point of the canvas in pixels, optional time) sets the key color of that material to the color of its surface under the point (the color map, the color of the file and the tint, before the key); comb (layer, name of a fur material, points: 2 to 4096 points of the canvas in pixels, radius in pixels, optional strength 0 to 1, hardness, erase, time) casts each point onto the model in the authoring view and paints, between each two points on that material, the direction of the drag along the surface, in the space of the model and times strength, into its comb map (maps.comb, a linear picture of 512 px under maps/, kept and painted over by later strokes; erase paints it back to the comb of the material; maps with comb: null clears it); new_material (name: writes effects/<name>.json and .wgsl from a template of a material shader and adds the effect); debug (optional show: mask, matte or shadows in ext.editor.debug3d, which the preview draws instead of the light; with layer and material only that material of that model layer shows it, as {show, layer, material}, and the others are drawn as they are; without show the preview lights again) model DIR add|set|remove|info …, shadows DIR LAYER [--on B] [--resolution N|--automatic] [--bias B] [--show], camera-path DIR add|key|remove|set|order|fade|show …, new-material DIR NAME [--check], debug3d DIR mask|matte|shadows|none, group3d DIR add|space|depth-test|show …, material DIR LAYER [NAME] … [--key COLOR] [--list], camera3d DIR …, view3d DIR …, tonemap DIR …, or scene3d DIR --model JSON (one of --model, --group, --space, --depth-test, --material, --paint, --camera, --view, --tones, --shadows, --path, --new-material, --debug, --key, --comb, --grab, --light, --sculpt) as the app sends it; model-info FILE.glb and cost3d DIR print what the inspector shows
