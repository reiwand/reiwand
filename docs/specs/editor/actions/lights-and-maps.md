# Lights and maps

| Action | Arguments | Command |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| `light` | one of `add` (`light`: `point`, `spot`, `tube` or `directional`, optional `id`, `into` a group and `at`, its position in that group; a new light starts in the middle of the canvas with the defaults of its type: a point light of radius 400 at a height of 120, a spot light of radius 900 at 150 with an intensity of 2, a cone of `[25, 45]` and a tilt of 45°, a tube light of length 400 and radius 240 at 60, a directional light with an intensity of 0.8 and a tilt of 50°; `add_layer` with `kind: light` and the type as `source` does the same), `set` (`layer` and any of `light`, which drops `cone`, `length` and `texture` where the new type has none, `color`, `intensity` 0 to 100, `radius` 1 to 100 000, `z`, `cone` `[inner, outer]` for spot lights, `length` for tube lights, `tilt` −89 to 89, `at`, a point of the canvas the light moves to, and `aim`, a point of the canvas it turns to, both through its parents as the light is placed, with its anchor and the skew of its parents, at `time` (0 by default); `color`, `intensity`, `radius`, `z`, `tilt`, `at` and `aim` write a key at `time` when that field or that part of the transform has keys, or replace them with `replace`; `cone` and `length` cannot have keys and are always written plain), `remove` (`layer`), `solo` (`layer`, or none to end it), `mute` (`layer`, `muted`), `volumetric` (`layer` of a point or spot light, optional `density` 0 to 1, `samples` 4 to 64 and `shafts` 0 to 1, starting from 0.2, 16 and 0, or `off`) and `projection` (`layer` of a spot light and one of `asset`, a picture or video of the scene, `from`, a layer that takes no light and reads none that does, or `off`); solo and mute are kept in `/scene/ext/editor/lights` and only change the preview; `set` also drops `volumetric` from tube and directional lights | `light DIR LAYER add [--type T] [--into G] [--at X,Y]`, `light DIR LAYER set [--type T] [--color C] [--intensity I] [--radius R] [--z Z] [--cone IN,OUT] [--length L] [--tilt D] [--at X,Y] [--aim X,Y]`, `light DIR LAYER remove`, `light DIR LAYER solo [--off]`, `light DIR LAYER mute [--off]`, `light DIR LAYER falloff [--time T]` (the points of the falloff curve), `light DIR LAYER volumetric [--density D] [--samples N] [--shafts S] [--off]` or `--show` (the glow against the distance, the size and samples each quality level keeps and on phones), `light DIR LAYER projection [--asset A \| --layer L] [--off]` or `--show` (the pictures, videos and layers it may project and where the picture lands on the canvas), and `lights DIR [--time T]` prints every light with its handles, its dirty box and the count against each device tier, or with `--cost [--quality Q]` what the lighting costs at each quality level |
| `lighting` | `layer` (an image, video, text, mesh, model or particles layer; a particles layer takes only `strength` and `emissive`), optional `normal`, `env`, `roughness_map` and `emissive_map` (pictures of the scene; an empty id removes one), `roughness`, `reflection` and `strength` (0 to 1), `emissive` (0 to 16) and `off` (removes `lighting`); the first change writes the block. A normal, roughness or glow map that is not `linear` becomes `linear` in the same step, or, when the picture is also drawn in colors, the field names a new asset `<id>_linear` of the same file that is; `particles … lit` and `refract` do the same with their normal maps | `lighting DIR LAYER [--normal ID] [--env ID] [--roughness-map ID] [--emissive-map ID] [--roughness R] [--reflection R] [--emissive E] [--strength S] [--off]` |
| `normals` | `asset` (a picture) or `layer` (an image layer, whose picture is used; a video layer, whose frame at `time` seconds is used, the first one by default and the last one past its end, picked in the dialog with a frame slider that starts at the playhead; or a text layer, whose letters are drawn at the size of the layer with its font, broken into lines at `wrap` and at line breaks as the renderer lays them out, with its `background` raised as a plate at half height under the letters, rounded by its radius), optional `strength` (0 to 16, 2 by default), `blur` (0 to 32 pixels, 1 by default) and `id` (refused when it names an asset that is not a normal map made here); makes a normal map from the edges of the brightness of the picture (a Sobel filter after a Gaussian blur, the same as `reiwand-depth` offers), saves it as `normals/<id>-<n>.png` (`.ktx2` with the codec and quality of the picture when that one is KTX2), declares it as a `linear` asset and, with `layer`, sets it as the `normal` of that layer | `normals DIR ASSET --generate [--strength S] [--blur B] [--layer L] [--id ID] [--time T]`, or `--preview` to print the relief, the map and a lit sphere without keeping anything |
| `paint_map` | `layer`, `map` (`roughness` or `emissive`), `radius`, optional `hardness` and `value` (the gray, 0 to 1) and `points` (fractions of the layer); paints the stroke into the map the layer has when it is a painted map (a `linear` picture under `maps/`) that nothing else uses, or else into a new map that starts as a copy of the one it had, or the size of its picture (at most 512 on the longer side), white for roughness and black for glow, saves it as `maps/<id>-<n>.png` (`.ktx2` with the codec and quality of the map it replaces, or else of the layer's picture, when that one is KTX2), declares it as a `linear` asset and sets `roughness_map` or `emissive_map` | `paint-map DIR LAYER roughness\|emissive --points X,Y;X,Y;... [--radius R] [--hardness H] [--value V]`, or `--heat` to print the map as a heat view |
| `ramp` | `stops` (1 to 16 of `{ "at": 0 to 1, "color" }`, in any order), optional `pointer` (a parameter of an effect that takes the result), `asset` (a ramp made here, to make it again from other stops; refused for any other asset), `width` (16 to 1024, 256 by default) and `format`: `png` (default) writes a picture of one row `ramps/<id>-<n>.png` read from left to right, as the `gradient` parameters of `fire`, `fluid`, `shine` and `light_shafts` do; `cube` writes a color table `luts/<id>-<n>.cube` of 17 steps that colors each tone by the ramp at its luminance, for `lut` parameters such as that of `color_lut`. It declares the asset, keeps the stops in `/scene/ext/editor/ramps/<id>` so that they can be edited again (query `{"ramp": "ASSET"}` answers them) and sets the parameter, all in one undoable step | `scene act DIR ramp JSON` |
| `depth_map` | `asset` (a picture of the scene), optional `pointer` (a parameter of an effect that takes the result), `id` (the asset made, by default `<asset>_depth`) and `input` (the model input, a multiple of 14 from 196 to 1036, 518 by default); estimates the depth of the picture with the depth model of the app, writes it as a grey `linear` picture `maps/<id>-<n>.png` (white is near) and sets the parameter. It is refused with the command that installs it when the depth model, or the library that runs it, is not installed (query `{"depth_model": {}}` answers `installed`, `runtime`, `ready` and `install`); the inspector offers it as *Make a depth map* next to the `depth` parameters, with a picker of the picture, and says why it is disabled while the model is missing | `scene act DIR depth_map JSON` |
| `ambient` | optional `ambient` and `sky` (colors), `clear` (removes both); sets `canvas.ambient` and `canvas.sky` | `ambient DIR [--ambient C] [--sky C] [--clear]`, or `--preview` to print the hemisphere they make |
