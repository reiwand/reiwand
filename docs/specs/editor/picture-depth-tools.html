The Depth page of the picture editor in the app and reiwand-ctl edit depth shape the depth layers that parallax moves (dbus.md, "Depth layers") through the depth section of the edit of a picture. They are actions of the edit, the same ones the app runs with editor.run and reiwand-ctl with a command, so every step has the undo history of the picture: each stroke, each change of the cuts and each mark is one step. All coordinates are fractions of the original picture, before the crop and the rotation, and a radius is a fraction of its longer side.

Action Fields reiwand-ctl edit depth …
depth_brush points (a stroke of 1 to 1 024 points, X,Y;X,Y;...), by (0 to 1, 0.1 by default: the depth added at the middle of the brush, fading to nothing at its edge), raise or lower (raise when neither), radius (above 0 and up to 1, 0.05 by default), falloff (0 hard to 1 soft, 0.5 by default: how much of the radius fades) and clear (removes the earlier strokes first, or alone removes them); adds the stroke to depth.strokes, which are applied in order to the adjusted depth map (after near, far, gamma, dilate and smooth), so every use of the map sees them: the depth masks, the regions and layers and the overlay. At most 256 strokes and 3 000 points in all brush FILE X,Y;X,Y;... [--raise|--lower] [--by V] [--radius R] [--falloff F] [--clear]
depth_cuts cuts (1 to 5 growing levels between 0.005 and 0.995, at least 0.005 apart, L,L,...) or auto; the regions of the picture (the connected parts between depth jumps, dbus.md, "Depth layers") are grouped into layers by their mean depth at those levels instead of at the ones the clustering finds, so a region is never torn (a layer left without pixels is dropped, and then the cut that bounded it), so the number of layers follows the cuts and not parallax.layers; auto goes back to the clustering cuts FILE L,L,... | --auto
fill_mask points, radius and falloff as the brush, refill or protect (one of them with points) and clear; marks, in depth.fills, where the band of a layer, the pixels behind nearer layers that moving can uncover, ignores its reach and is always filled (refill), or where it is never filled (protect, the pixels stay transparent in the layers behind); a later mark paints over an earlier one where they meet fill-mask FILE X,Y;X,Y;... (--refill|--protect) [--radius R] [--falloff F] [--clear]
depth_join points (2 to 8 points, X,Y;X,Y;..., one inside each region to make one) or clear; adds to depth.joins one join of the first point with each of the others, so the regions under them become one region, share a layer and move together; clear removes the earlier joins first, or alone removes them. At most 64 joins join FILE X,Y;X,Y;... [--clear]
depth_split points (a stroke of 1 to 1 024 points), radius (half the width of the cut as a fraction of the longer side, 0.004 by default) and clear; adds a hard stroke to depth.splits: the pixels it covers are a jump, so a region it crosses falls in two regions that can take different layers. At most 64 splits split FILE X,Y;X,Y;... [--radius R] [--clear]
depth_move at (X,Y, a point inside the region), by (-1 to 1: above 0 nearer, below 0 farther) and clear; adds to depth.moves a move of the whole region under at, after the regions are found: its depth, its layer and its movement follow; clear puts every moved region back first, or alone puts them back. At most 64 moves move FILE X,Y --by V [--clear]

The joins, splits and moves of depth (dbus.md, "Edits and spanning") merge two regions, cut one along a stroke or move one as a whole; edit depth regions FILE [--png FILE] [--json] lists the regions from the nearest with a point inside each (the ones to give to join and move) and saves them painted, blue far to red near. The layers (reiwand-ctl edit depth layers) and the key of their cache follow the strokes, cuts, marks, joins, splits and moves, so a change builds a new set and the old one is trimmed with the cache. The map of the app and edit depth export --adjusted include the strokes. edit depth show FILE --layer N --out PNG saves one layer with its transparency, --band PNG also saves the band of that layer, white on black, computed again with the cuts, strokes and marks of the set, and --fill lama|simple picks the set made with that fill instead of the newest. edit depth compare FILE --out PNG [--layer N] [--at X] builds, when they are missing, the set filled by LaMa and the one filled by the simple fill, with progress on the standard error, and saves one layer with LaMa on the left of X (0.5 by default) and the simple fill on the right; it refuses to run while LaMa is not installed.

In the app the Depth tool of the picture editor, once the picture has a depth map, shows the whole picture, unturned and uncropped, so that what is painted lines up with the original (the crop, the rotation and the straightening come back with the other tools), and offers:

  • Show the depth map over the picture: the adjusted map, strokes included, in grey or in false colour, over the picture with its own opacity (Relief.look, drawn once per change of the edit and never per frame).
  • Paint depth: the brush for depth_brush, with raise or lower, size, strength and soft edge. The canvas draws the circle of the brush and the stroke while the button is down, thinned to a point every quarter of the radius, and sends the stroke on release as one depth_brush; undo removes it alone. Remove the strokes is depth_brush with clear.
  • Layer cuts: a strip with the histogram of the depth, far on the left, coloured by layer, and a handle for each cut that groups the regions into layers (the manual ones of depth.cuts, else the ones of the newest set or the ones the clustering would find). Dragging a handle keeps it at least 0.01 from its neighbours and draws, with Relief.trace, the outline of every layer tinted on the picture, at 512 pixels and without building anything; releasing it sends one depth_cuts, and the layers are built again with those cuts half a second after the last change, a newer change cancelling the build in progress. A double click on a handle removes it, a click on the strip or Add a cut adds one in the widest gap (at most 5), and Back to automatic is depth_cuts with auto. The line under the strip counts the layers and the regions of the set, and when the picture stays still it says why in one line (no depth map, fill off, set being built, failed build, or depth that gives no movement without stretching); reiwand-ctl edit depth layers prints the same line on the standard error after building and --list shows the regions, the size, the safe shift and whether each set moves.
  • Fill brush: the same brush for fill_mask, with Refill and Protect, size and soft edge (no strength: a mark is all or nothing where it covers at least half of the brush). The marks show in the layer views as the band they widen or empty, the layers are built again after the last stroke, and Remove the marks is fill_mask with clear.
  • Layers and fill: a list of the layers, from the farthest, that shows one alone over the picture (Relief.view), with Highlight the band that moving can uncover tinting the pixels the set filled (the --band of edit depth show), and Compare LaMa with the simple fill, which shows the LaMa layer on the left of a divider that is dragged and the simple one on the right (edit depth compare). A set that is missing is built then, with its progress and a Cancel button, and the comparison is offered only while LaMa and ONNX Runtime are installed.

The Parallax tool of the picture editor, once the picture has a depth map, shows the whole picture as the Depth tool does and adds the Regions group, the author tools for the regions of the picture:

  • Show the regions on the picture: every region painted over the picture, blue the farthest to red the nearest, with a number at a point inside it that is its place in the order of depth (1 the nearest). The chart (Relief.regionsOf, reiwand_depth::chart, at 512 pixels, drawn once per change of the edit) also gives each region its depth, its share of the picture and a coarse grid of the order of each cell, which is what a click on the picture reads.
  • Picking: a click on a region of the picture, or a tick in the list under the switch (each row reachable with Tab), picks it; a second click drops it. Picked regions are tinted white.
  • Join: with two or more regions picked, sends one depth_join with a point of each; Nearer and Farther send one depth_move per picked region, by the amount of Move by; Put the regions back is the three clear actions (depth_join, depth_split and depth_move).
  • Split along a stroke: while on, a drag on the picture draws a stroke (thinned to a point every 0.3 % of the picture, 1 000 points at most) and sends it on release as one depth_split.
  • Straight split of the picked region: with one region picked, a dashed line along the longer side of its bounds, moved across with Where to cut (a slider, so the arrow keys move it), and Cut along the line sends it as one depth_split; it is the keyboard way to split.
  • Marks that no longer apply: reiwand_depth::stale checks every saved mark against the regions of the current depth map: a join whose points are already in one region, a split whose stroke crosses no region (taking it out leaves the same number of regions) and a move whose point is in a region that an earlier move or join already took. Such a move is not applied (the first one stays) and none of them fails the build; the tool lists them by number and edit depth regions prints them on the standard error, so they can be cleared or redone.
  • Undo and Redo are the undo and redo of the picture, so each join, split and move is one step.
  • Preview the movement while you edit: once the layers of the current edit are built, a CPU rendering of the layers at 480 pixels (reiwand_depth::Preview, the same travel limit, field lookups and zoom as the desktop, Relief.peek) replaces the painted regions on the canvas. The pointer is the mouse over the picture, the dot of a pad (arrow keys move it when it has focus) or an orbit; each new position replaces the one waiting, never queues, and nothing runs while the switch is off.
  • The movement limit: the line Movement limit gives the safe shift of the layers of the current edit as a share of the width. When a change lowers it by 3 % or more, a warning says which change did it and the limit before and after; it goes when the limit comes back (undo) or on Dismiss.
  • Progress of the layers: a bar at the top of the tool follows the build the app starts after each change and the layers job of the daemon, with Cancel for the app's own build.

The settings page of the app has, next to the LaMa download, Check LaMa (reiwand-ctl models verify lama-big-fp32), Remove LaMa (models remove lama-big-fp32) and Rebuild the layers, which deletes every layer set of the cache (the same as edit depth layers --clear for each picture) and reloads the daemon so that it builds those of the pictures it shows; the button shows how many sets are kept, or the progress of the layers job of the daemon while one runs.
