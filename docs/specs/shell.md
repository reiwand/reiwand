# Window of the desktop app

The window (`apps/reiwand-app/qml/Frame.qml`) puts the wallpaper in use edge to edge, the menu of
words on the left and each page in one of three skeletons. Pages do not draw a sidebar, a global
bar or a Kirigami title of their own: the skeleton takes care of it.

The specification, part by part:

- [Image and tones, Menu, Skeletons, Keyboard, Choosing files and folders, Notices, Size and What a page declares](shell/image-and-tones.md): image and tones, menu, skeletons, keyboard, choosing files and folders, notices, size, what a page declares.
- [Low band of the title screens, Sources and Settings](shell/low-band-of-the-title-screens.md): low band of the title screens, sources, settings.
- [Private mode, Name of the screens, Now block, Lists and Third-party licenses](shell/private-mode.md): private mode, name of the screens, now block, lists, third-party licenses.
