# `io.github.reiwand.Daemon1`: `SetOption`

On this page: `setoption`.

## `SetOption(s key, s value)`

Changes one setting and saves the configuration. Keys and their current values are listed in
`GetState().options`, and described in `GetSchema().options`:

| Key | Values | Default |
|-----|--------|---------|
| `playback.volume` | `0.0` to `1.0`, for videos and scenes with sound | `0.5` |
| `playback.muted` | `true`, `false`, for videos and scenes with sound | `true` |
| `playback.max_fps` | `1` to `240`, or `display` (also `0`) for the refresh rate of each output, up to 60 on battery (see "Frame rate") | `display` |
| `playback.software_parallax` | move depth parallax images even when the GPU is emulated on the CPU (such as llvmpipe), with 8 steps at 15 frames per second at most | `false` |
| `playback.allow_flashing` | play the packages marked as photosensitive; while it is off they are listed with the notice `flashes and is disabled` (`PackageInfo.flash_disabled`), `Apply` refuses them with `FlashingDisabled` and playlists and rotation skip them; the setting is off by default and nothing asks per wallpaper. While it is off the compositor also measures the brightness of every scene frame and slow down, or stop, a scene that flashes more than three times a second (see "Cost and frame rate" in `scene.md`); it costs a small read back from the GPU per frame | `false` |
| `playback.scene_sound` | let scenes play their sound layers (see "Scene sound"); `false` keeps every scene silent, whatever its permissions and volume, and leaves the sound of videos alone | `true` |
| `playback.scene_capture` | let scenes that were granted `audio` follow the sound that plays (see "Inputs"); `false` closes the audio capture for every scene | `true` |
| `playback.auto_pace` | how the automatic quality of the daemon, of phones and of `reiwand-ctl scene bench --quality auto` uses the frame rate (see "Cost and frame rate" in `scene.md`): `detail` may halve the pace of a heavy scene, never under 30 frames a second and never under a lower user cap, to keep a higher level; `smooth` never changes it. A fixed quality level is never touched; on the desktop the halved pace halves the rate of that output | `detail` |
| `playback.quality` | the quality level of scenes: `auto` lets the engine choose it from the cost of the scene, the GPU class, the GPU time of the frames, battery, power saving and heat (see "Cost and frame rate" in `scene.md`); `q0` to `q3` fix it, and `min-q1` or `min-q2` keep the automatic choice but never below that level; a level the user sets holds on battery, with power saving, while warm and over the cost, and a heavy scene shows fewer frames a second instead, also one that `auto` keeps still because it is too heavy for the device; it is not synced and profiles leave it out | `auto` |
| `playback.hdr` | HDR output (`auto`, `on` or `off`): on a screen whose surface the graphics driver can present in extended linear sRGB (half floats, which on Wayland the driver announces through the color management protocol of the desktop), or else in PQ (BT.2100, 10 bits, encoded by the daemon from the same linear picture with the white of the screen as its reference), or else, when the driver offers neither but the desktop takes parametric image descriptions with the PQ transfer and BT.2020 primaries, in 10 bits that the daemon encodes in PQ itself and describes to the desktop with `wp_color_management_surface_v1` (with MaxCLL, the brightest light it may draw: the peak of the screen, or twice its white when it says nothing; MaxFALL, its white; and, when the desktop takes them, BT.709 mastering primaries and that range as mastering luminance), scenes keep their colors brighter than white and tone map them to the peak brightness of that screen, read from the preferred image description the desktop gives the surface (its target or maximum luminance over its reference white; twice the white when the desktop says nothing); `auto` does it only when the desktop says the screen shows HDR, on mains power and while the scene of the screen has `canvas.hdr` with a tone map and draws at `q3` or the screen shows an HDR video or an HDR KTX2 picture, `on` whenever the surface can even if the desktop says nothing, `off` never; other screens, streams and X11 keep standard colors. With HDR on, an HDR video (PQ or HLG) keeps its highlights above white and is tone mapped to the peak of the screen (the lowest peak among the screens that show it), an HDR KTX2 picture (UASTC HDR, shown without photo edits) keeps its light above white, bent softly toward the peak of that screen from half its white up, and other pictures, standard videos and web pages, made for standard range, keep their white at the white of the screen; the phone does the same for its pictures | `auto` |
| `playback.hdr.<output>` | the same values for one output, or `none` to follow `playback.hdr`; kept as `hdr` in `[outputs."<output>"]`, not listed in `options` but in `hdrs`, read back with `reiwand-ctl config playback.hdr.<output>` and kept when a profile is used | none |
| `playback.quality.<output>` | the same values for the scenes and web pages of one output, or `none` (or an empty value) to follow `playback.quality` again; kept as `quality` in `[outputs."<output>"]`, not listed in `options` but in `qualities`, read back with `reiwand-ctl config playback.quality.<output>` even while the output is not connected, kept when a profile is used; outputs that show the same page share one helper while the choice they end up with is the same, whether their own or the general one, so a page reads the level of the output that shows it, and a page whose choice changes without splitting keeps its helper and only hears the new level | none |
| `playback.duck` | fade the sound of every wallpaper (videos, scene sounds and web pages) out in 0.5 s while another application plays sound, and back in when it stops; a PipeWire watcher counts the running playback streams of other processes (not the daemon, its helpers or their children), reading neither their names nor their sound | `false` |
| `playback.lowest_below_percent` | `0` to `100`: draw scenes at the lowest quality (`q0`) while running on battery with a charge under this percentage (`0` turns it off); it applies as set, also over a fixed `playback.quality` and the quality of one output, and does nothing while the charge is unknown | `0` |
| `transition.kind` | `own` (each wallpaper plays the transition it brings, or the plain fade when neither brings one), `fade` (always the plain fade), `none` (a cut, also when a playlist sets `transition_ms`) | `own` |
| `transition.duration_ms` | `0` to `10000` | `600` |
| `shortcuts.enabled` | `true`, `false`: ask the desktop for the keys that browse wallpapers | `false` |
| `shortcuts.next` | keys joined by `+`, such as `LOGO+ALT+N`; a proposal the desktop may change | `LOGO+ALT+N` |
| `shortcuts.previous` | the same, to browse back | `LOGO+ALT+P` |
| `shortcuts.apply` | the same, to apply the candidate of the open session | `LOGO+ALT+Return` |
| `shortcuts.cancel` | the same, to close the session without changing | `LOGO+ALT+Escape` |
| `transition.kind.<output>` | the same values for one output, or `general` (or an empty value) to follow `transition.kind`; kept as `transition` in `[outputs."<output>"]`, not listed in `options` but in `transitions`, read back with `reiwand-ctl config transition.kind.<output>` and kept when a profile is used | general |
| `transition.less_motion` | `system` (follow the desktop's reduced motion), `always`, `never`: while it reduces, every transition becomes the plain fade and `none` stays a cut; it does not touch parallax | `system` |
| `pause.when_covered` | pause while a maximized or fullscreen window covers the output | `true` |
| `pause.on_battery` | one setting for pausing on battery, with three values: `never`, `always` (pause whenever the machine runs on battery) and `below:N` with `N` from `1` to `100` (pause on battery with a charge under `N` percent; it applies as set, whatever `playback.quality` says, and does nothing while the charge is unknown, `battery_percent` being `null`); the key belongs to each device and is never synced (a value set on one device does not change how another behaves); the phone reads the same key and its default is `never`; a file that still has the old boolean `on_battery` or the old `below_percent` key is not valid: the daemon reports it in `config_error`, keeps a copy of the file and runs with the defaults until the setting is written again | `always` |
| `pause.when_locked` | pause while the screen is locked; whatever it says, videos are muted and scenes play no sound while it is locked | `true` |
| `library.watch` | rescan folder sources when files in them change (a new file once it is written, a hard or symbolic link, a moved, renamed or removed folder), one scan per source at most every 5 s; a change during a scan of the same source starts another scan after it | `true` |
| `library.thumb_cache_mb` | `64` to `8192`, size of the thumbnail cache | `512` |
| `library.hide_mature` | leave packages rated `mature` out of `Search` (unless the query has its own `content:` filter; `SearchPage.query` stays the one given) and `Browse`; a change emits `library` and `repos` | `false` |
| `library.show_mature` | show the previews of packages rated `mature` without blur; otherwise clients (app, tray, phone) blur them until the user confirms once per package with `Library1.Confirm`; a change emits `library` and `repos` | `false` |
| `repos.auto_refresh` | refresh each repository 10 min after start and then every 24 h; the phone reads it for its daily check | `true` |
| `repos.auto_update` | after each refresh, update without asking the installed packages that have a newer version in their repository, with every check of `Install`; a version that asks for a permission the installed one lacks (an input, `rgb` or a network origin) is not installed: it stays in `ListInstalled` with `asks`. Off by default, because updating without asking changes what runs on the device. There is no notification for updates either way | `false` | On the desktop the automatic update waits for mains power (it does not run on battery and runs when plugged in). The repository index lists the permissions of each version (`asks`); if a new version asks for one that the installed one lacks, it is held back without downloading it. On the phone, when updating only the new version is recorded. "Only on another device": the note is recomputed on installing or importing without waiting for synchronization, and a package from a repository not added here says which one it is (`repo`); the phone does not synchronize profiles, which is why it has no note on screen. |
| `repos.vote_counts` | fetch and show vote counts from the linked server in the catalog (`GetVotes`); voting itself stays possible | `true` |
| `layout.bezel_mm` | `0` to `100`, frame width of every screen side for the `span` fit | `0.0` |
| `layout.monitors.<screen>` | `x,y,w,h` in millimetres places the screen whose identity or output name is `<screen>` on the canvas of the `span` fit (see "Edits and spanning"), `none` or an empty value lets the daemon place it again; at most 64 screens, positions within ±100 m and sizes up to 10 m; not listed in `options` | none |
| `layout.follow.<output>` | another output name: `<output>` shows the part of a `span` wallpaper or spread scene that the named output shows, also when `<output>` has no place on the desktop (streams, previews); one step only, an output never follows itself; `none` or an empty value clears it; at most 64; not listed in `options`, `GetState().outputs[x].follows` tells it | none |
| `models.allow_download` | let `reiwand-ctl models download` and the app download the depth model and ONNX Runtime after asking | `true` |
| `rgb.enabled` | send the colors of packages granted `rgb` to RGB lighting through the OpenRGB server (see `Rgb1`) | `false` |
| `rgb.when_covered` | what the lights do while a window covers the output they follow and the wallpaper pauses for it: `pause` sends nothing, samples nothing and leaves the devices with their last colors until the output shows again (with `pause.when_covered` off the covered wallpaper keeps drawing and the lights keep following it, as before this key existed); `follow` keeps the lights following the scene or page while covered: a scene paused by `pause.when_covered` keeps running at most 30 times a second, drawn at most 320 pixels a side for the strip and never to the screen or the stream, without those frames changing its quality level, and a page keeps running at the same rate without being shown, both only while the server is connected and has devices (otherwise the covered wallpaper stays paused and, while it holds the `rgb` grant, sends colors and nothing else pauses it, the daemon tries the server again every 10 seconds); synced like the other `rgb` keys | `pause` |
| `media.remote_covers` | download covers that media players give as `http` or `https` addresses (at most 4 MiB, within 5 s, kept in memory only); turning it on fetches the cover of the track that plays at once, and turning it off drops the covers fetched that way; off, only `file://` covers are read. Kept on each device, never synced | `false` |
| `network.private` | private mode: cuts all network of the program (see `SetPrivateMode`); everything on the computer keeps working. Kept on each device, never synced | `false` |
| `updates.auto` | the daily check for a new version that the AppImage installs (`reiwand --update --auto`): it asks GitHub for the latest release and, when it is newer and its signature is the release key's, installs it. Private mode stops it too. Stored in the optional `[updates]` block (without it, on). Kept on each device, never synced | `true` |
| `rgb.place.<device>[#<zone>]` | the part of the strip a device, or one zone of it (`#` and its index from 0), takes: `x y w h` as fractions of the strip from 0 to 1 (inside it, `w` and `h` above 0) and then `right` (default), `left`, `down` or `up` for the way a line of lights runs, or `off` to leave the device alone or keep the zone dark; `none` or an empty value takes the whole strip again; device keys (the `key` of `Rgb1.GetStatus().devices`: the name, with the serial or order in parentheses when devices share it) of 1 to 128 characters without control characters, at most 256 places; not listed in `options`, kept on this computer and never synced, `Rgb1.GetStatus().places` tells them | none |
| `rgb.server` | `host:port` of the OpenRGB server, at most 255 characters; an address that is not this computer sends the colors over the network | `127.0.0.1:6742` |
| `render.gpu` | the graphics card the daemon renders with: `auto` (the one that saves the most power, an integrated card before a dedicated one), `integrated`, `dedicated`, `software` (emulated on the processor, for tests) or part of the name of a card, without regard to case, such as `Graphics A` (see "Graphics card"); it takes effect the next time the daemon starts, it is not synced and profiles leave it out | `auto` |
| `surfaces.layer_namespace` | the namespace of the layer-shell surfaces (see "Layer-shell and X11 outputs"): `auto` (or an empty value) for the automatic choice, or 1 to 64 ASCII letters, digits, `-`, `_` or `.`; it takes effect the next time the daemon starts, it is not synced and profiles leave it out | `auto` |

Only the changed key is validated. Unknown keys and invalid or out-of-range values return
`InvalidArgs` and change nothing.
