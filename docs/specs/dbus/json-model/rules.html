The Rust types are in crates/reiwand-core/src/model: none has serde(default) or skip_serializing_if, and every Option is read through explicit::null (a key that is present holds a value or null; absent is an error). crates/reiwand-core/prefill/model.tsv lists each optional field (Type.field) with its class: block (an optional block, a nested object or null: in a stored file it may be absent, in a reply the daemon writes it) or value (a single value that is always written, null for none). The test tests/model_explicit.rs fails when a field is missing from the table or a default comes back. The messages that clients send are not part of this model: they are the typed arguments of each method and the JSON of SetSceneProperty, PreviewEdit and scene act, which change only what they name ("change only this"), by design. Adding a field, an enum value or a method keeps the model version; removing, renaming or retyping something increases it. Times are Unix seconds; Fit is "fill", "fit", "stretch", "center", "tile" or "span"; enums are written in snake case. edited tells whether an edit applies to the shown image and screen repeats SetOutputInfo; mm is [x, y, w, h], where the screen sits on the canvas in millimetres (see "Edits and spanning"), or null before SetOutputInfo. calibrating tells whether ShowCalibration shows its pattern. parallax tells whether the image moves in depth; while it does not and its edit asks for it, still says why (no_model, no_map, edited, building, building_simple, failed, cancelled or flat, see "Depth layers"), else null; cancelled follows a CancelJob of its layers job; building_simple is a build that uses the simple fill because LaMa is not installed. A picture whose layers are built but that the computer draws with its processor reports software until playback.software_parallax is set. page_errors lists the errors a web page wrote since it started (its console lines that are not plain logging, the latest 50 at most); the library marks the item of that page as broken with the latest one, and reiwand-ctl state prints them. frames counts the frames rendered for the output since it appeared; page_frames counts the frames the web page of the output drew (0 for other wallpapers), which the frame rate cap limits. refresh is the refresh rate of its screen in Hz (0 while unknown) and fps the most frames per second it renders now (see "Frame rate"). buffers is "dmabuf" or "memfd" while a consumer streams the output, and "" otherwise (see "Stream buffers"). sound is "", "off", "playing", "paused" or "elsewhere" (see "Scene sound"). decode is "zero-copy" when the video or web page of that output reaches the GPU as a DMA-BUF that it reads in place, "copy" when its frames go through system memory, and "" for other content (see "Video decoding" and web.md). sandbox is "" for other content, "on" while a web page runs with every layer of its sandbox, and "reduced" when the web engine could not start its own sandbox or the page could not get its own empty network, so clients can warn about it. helper is the process id of the web helper that draws the page of that output, and null for other content or while no helper runs. flashes is "", or "slowed" or "paused" once the guard of playback.allow_flashing (off) slowed or stopped the scene of that output. left_out lists the package effects the scene of that output plays without, as the effect <id> was left out: <reason>, and the uses the hard budget drew with their lighter variant as the effect <id> on <layer> was drawn with its lighter variant: <reason>; it is a note, not a failure. particles is "gpu" or "cpu" for a scene with particles, and "" otherwise. quality is the quality level the scene of that output draws at ("q0" to "q3", "" for other content) and quality_reason why: "full" (the highest), "device" (the most its graphics card class allows), "cost" (the scene is too heavy for the card at a higher level), "battery", "saver" (power saving), "heat", "slow" (its frames took too long on the GPU) or "user" (playback.quality fixed the level or kept it above a floor); see "Cost and frame rate" in scene.md. quality_choice is the own value of playback.quality.<output> or null when the output follows playback.quality; quality_cost and quality_budget are the units a frame the scene costs at its level and the units the GPU class holds, both 0 unless the user's choice (a fixed level or a floor) put the level where it costs more than the class holds, in which case clients warn that frames may drop and battery use rise. quality_auto says what the automatic choice does or would do with the scene: "still" (the scene is too heavy for the class and stays at its poster_time because the automatic choice is in charge), "stop" (a fixed level or a floor plays a scene that auto would keep still, also when only its model triangles exceed the class and its cost fits, in which case clients warn that it draws more model triangles than the device holds, whatever the reason of the level), "lower" (auto would only lower the level: with the user's choice, the frames measured at that level take more than 85 % of their interval, or, before 12 frames are measured, the level costs more than the class holds) or "", also when the estimate is over budget but the measured frames fit. quality_frame_ms is the average GPU time of the last frames at that level (null until 12 are measured, and again for a moment after each change), quality_period_ms the interval it is judged against (that of the pace in use), quality_step the last decision of the automatic choice (model, steady, climbed, dropped, top, held, still, halved or doubled, see "Cost and frame rate" in scene.md; "" for other content) and quality_pace its pace divisor (1, or 2 while it plays at half the rate, 0 for other content); reiwand-ctl quality shows them in its MEASURED column and the app and the tray under the level of each screen. With a measure, clients say how long the frames take against the interval, and the rate it allows, instead of the estimate. Clients tell a still scene from a playing one by this value, never by quality_reason, which keeps the automatic reason (or "slow") when a floor does not raise the level. tier is the class of the graphics card, "t0" to "t3", and tier_measured whether a short measured render set it rather than the model of the card alone. adapters lists the graphics cards the daemon finds, at start and again a second after a card is plugged in or taken out, each with its name, kind ("integrated", "dedicated", "virtual", "software" or "other"), backend ("vulkan" or "gl"), used, true for the one it renders with, and bus, its PCI bus id in lower case (empty when the driver gives none) and compositor, true for the card the compositor renders with (none while the daemon does not know it). gpu_note says, in the language of the daemon, that the compositor renders with another card than the one in use, so every frame is copied through the processor (a card forced by render.gpu, or the fallback), or is null; the app shows it under the card picker and reiwand-ctl gpu prints it with a DESKTOP column. gpu_warning says, in the language of the daemon, that no card matched render.gpu, or that the matching card could not be opened, and the automatic choice was taken, or is null; gpu_restart is true while render.gpu differs from the value the daemon started with, without regard to case. qualities maps every output that has its own playback.quality.<output> in the configuration, connected or not, to that value, and hdrs does the same for playback.hdr.<output>. hdr of an output tells its HDR output: choice (the value in use), own (its own playback.hdr.<output> or null), active (it presents brighter than white now), offered (its surface can), display (the desktop says the screen shows HDR), peak_nits and reference_nits (from the desktop, or null), encoding ("scrgb", "pq", "pq-own" for the description of its own, "sdr", or empty on streams), max_cll and max_fall (the metadata of that description, null otherwise) and reason: "auto", "on", "off", "display" (the screen, the desktop or the driver offers no HDR, with auto also when the desktop does not say the screen shows HDR, and always on streams), "battery" or "quality" (no scene with canvas.hdr and a tone map is shown, or it draws below q3). previews holds, only for the preview: outputs of the scene editor (which outputs leaves out) that have either, their error and the console: the last 50 lines their scripts wrote, as <level> <module>: <text>; other outputs have no console so that scripts that write often never wake the bus. transitions maps every output that has its own transition.kind.<output>, connected or not, to that value. transition of an output tells: choice (the value in use there), own (its own transition.kind.<output> or null), plays (what played at the last change of wallpaper on it: own, fade or none) and why: "own" (the wallpaper's transition), "chosen" (the plain fade or the cut that was chosen, also a playlist with transition_ms 0), "reduced" (less motion turned it into the plain fade), "fallen" (the wallpaper's transition could not be built or drawn, or was too costly for the quality, and the plain fade played), "plain" (neither wallpaper brings a transition) or "cut" (no change yet, or the screen could not fade). reiwand-ctl config transition.kind prints one line per output with its choice and why.
