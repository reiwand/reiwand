The model layer#

A model layer (model3d) draws a binary glTF model with metallic-roughness materials. Alone it renders into its own box (prefilled size, the canvas) with depth, and then composes like any layer, with its effects, masks, blending and transform; a group with space: "3d" renders all its models in one pass with a shared depth into a box the size of the canvas, placed where a child of the group without a transform would be, which then composes as one layer in the place of the group. Models also take clips, animations, ik, physics, rules, shapes, expressions and hit as meshes do (model_animation, see "Animated models" below), and:

Field Meaning
model the .glb asset
position, rotation, scale three numbers each (or one for all three), in the units of the model and degrees (turned around x, then y, then z), which may be bound
root_motion true lets the animation of the root move the layer; needs clips
env a picture reflected by the materials, read as an equirectangular panorama: blurred for the light around and sharper for shiny materials; the lowest quality keeps only the light around
materials per material name of the file, at most 64 of { "shader", "color", "emissive", "lit", "double_sided", "cast_shadows", "receive_shadows", "cull": "back" | "front" | "none", "depth_test", "depth_write", "blend": "opaque" | "mask" | "blend" | "add", "maps", "params" }

Materials and shaders#

The materials of the file give the color, metal, roughness, normal, occlusion and glow maps with their factors (KHR_materials_emissive_strength multiplies the glow), the alpha mode and cutoff, and both sides; KHR_materials_unlit draws a material without light. A material of the scene multiplies its color by color, replaces the glow by emissive, picks shader and overrides the rest; lit: false draws it unlit, blend or an opacity below 1 draws it after the opaque ones, farthest first. Primitives without a material take a white one. Textures may be PNG, JPEG or WebP pictures of at most 4096 pixels; phones and the T0 and T1 tiers draw them at most 2048 pixels wide.

shader is always written: pbr (the editor suggests it; params metallic, roughness, occlusion 0 to 1 and normal_scale 0 to 10), unlit, fur (layers 1 to 32, length 0 to 100, density 0 to 1), foliage (strength and speed 0 to 10, swaying with canvas.wind), chroma (key color, threshold and softness 0 to 1) or the id of a package effect with stage: "material" (material_shaders for all but pbr and unlit); fur also takes comb_x, comb_y and comb_z from −1 to 1, and all but unlit take the numbers of pbr. Every parameter of fur, foliage and chroma listed here is written (the editor suggests layers 12, length 10, density 0.5, comb_x, comb_y and comb_z 0, strength and speed 1, key #00ff00, threshold 0.4 and softness 0.1); the numbers of pbr may stay out to keep those of the model file. Shadows of lights and materials name shadows; both cast_shadows and receive_shadows are true unless set, which changes nothing until a light casts shadows. maps replaces maps of the file with pictures of the scene: { "base", "orm", "normal", "occlusion", "emissive" } (and gives fur its comb map), each the id of a picture asset; a replaced map keeps the texture coordinates, the transform and the sampler of the slot it replaces (the first set unmoved, repeated and smooth where the file had no map) and should be linear for orm, normal and occlusion. A picture that is not loaded leaves the map of the file.

  • fur draws its primitives again as shells pushed out along the normal, layers of them (12 by default) at Q3, three quarters at Q2, half at Q1 and a quarter at Q0 (at least one); length is how far the outer shell stands, in hundredths of the radius of the model (prefilled 10), density how close the strands grow (0.5), and the comb bends them towards that direction of the model, more at the tips. maps.comb names a linear picture over the first texture coordinates that combs each place on its own: its red, green and blue are the direction in the space of the model (0.5 is none, 0 and 1 are −1 and 1) and its alpha how much it replaces the comb of the material; the vertex stage of the shells reads one texel of it per vertex (of a 1-pixel picture without a comb map), at every quality level and tier. Strands thin out towards the tips and shade darker at their roots; the shells are cut, not blended, so they need no sorting.
  • foliage sways the vertices with canvas.wind: the red of the vertex colors (COLOR_0) of the file says how much each vertex moves (black stays), or, without vertex colors, the height within the model. strength (prefilled 1) scales the sway, which reaches a twentieth of the radius of the model for a wind of 100 pixels a second, and speed (1) its pace. Without wind, at the lowest quality or when the wind stays still at nothing, foliage stays still and asks for no frames.
  • chroma makes the colors close to key (prefilled green, an sRGB color like the others, compared with the color of the surface in linear light) transparent: closer than threshold (0.4) is cut, and the cut fades over softness (0.1) beyond it.
  • A material shader of the package (stage: "material", checked and limited like every package shader) gives fn material(s: Surface) -> Surface, which runs for each pixel after the maps are read and before the light, and may change the place, normal, color, glow, roughness, metal and occlusion. It may read frame, param(0u) to param(7u) (its parameters in their order, set by params), the noise and color helpers, but no pictures. A shader that cannot be built is drawn as pbr, and the stage renders again each frame while it reads time, audio or the pointer.
