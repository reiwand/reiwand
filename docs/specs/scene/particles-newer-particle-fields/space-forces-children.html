Space, points, shapes and starting values#

The newer fields behave as follows.

  • World space. A world system moves in canvas pixels: the transform of its layer places, turns and scales particles, their speed and their shapes when they are born, and they are drawn without it, so moving the layer afterwards leaves them where they are. Gravity and the other forces act along the directions of the canvas, and targets, colliders and control points of the layer move with it. Each layer that draws a world system moves particles of its own.
  • Control points. at is in pixels of the layer, or of the canvas with space: world; a point that follows the cursor goes back to the center of the canvas when the pointer leaves. A shape with at is drawn around that point, turned by its angle. The points of a layer's instance and the point(n, [x, y]) of scripts move points p0 to p7 for that layer; a null entry of points leaves that point to the system. Without points, p0 is the pixel (0, 0) of the layer, the top-left corner of its frame and not the middle of the canvas that the layer turns around; it moves, turns and scales with the layer in both spaces, exactly like a target or center given as [0, 0]. To aim at the middle of the system, give p0 an at of half the canvas.
  • Shapes. inner cuts a box of that size out of the middle of a box, which births fill evenly. scale stretches a circle into an ellipse and sign keeps its positive (1) or negative (−1) half along x and along y. A sphere draws the three coordinates of a point of the ball and uses its z as depth only with perspective: the depth of a birth is init.depth plus depth of the shape plus the z of the point (the same rule as around, so a system that wants only the ball has no init.depth, or one of [0, 0]), and a system without perspective drops z, which leaves births spread as the picture of a ball seen from the front, thicker towards the middle than a disc (the share of births inside 0.6 of the radius is 0.49, against 0.36 for a disc). The x and y of the point go through space and the control point at like those of a circle, so they are turned and scaled with the placement; z is not. push moves each point v of the ball (after inner and sign) to v + push·v/|v|, the length of v taken over x, y and z together, so a point keeps its direction and side and the middle gets an empty hole; a point at the very middle (v = 0) stays there. With rz 0 the result is a disc with a round hole of radius push whose births keep the spread of a ball seen from the front: the share at a normalised distance ρ from the hole (0 at the hole, 1 at the edge) has the density 3ρ√(1 − ρ²). Unlike inner of a circle (even by area, so loaded towards the edge) or of a sphere (a shell that leaves nothing when the ball is flat), it keeps the hole with any depth radius. The radius for the speed given by radial is measured from the middle in the plane. tilt of init and the turn of around do not depend on it. Both paths draw the same point from the same random numbers.
  • Starting values. A range [min, max, exponent] picks min + (max − min) · u^exponent. alpha replaces the alpha of the color. hsv picks the hue in degrees (with steps, one of that many hues from one end of the range to the other), the saturation and the value from 0 to 1 (prefilled [1, 1]); palette picks one of its colors and moves its hue by up to noise[0] half turns and its saturation and value by up to noise[1] and noise[2]. radial adds a speed away from the center of the shape (the center of the canvas for edge and mask, the direction of angle for a point), and inherit adds the velocity of the pointer or of a control point, times a number picked from speed.

Forces, emitters and children#

  • Forces. window fades a force in between its first two fractions of the life and out between the last two. attractor, vortex and orbit weaken with distance as the inverse square, the inverse and not at all by default; with a radius they do nothing beyond it and weaken as 1 − d / radius (linear) or its square (inverse_square). A vortex with inner and outer turns fully inside inner and fades out at outer, and its ring pulls particles toward a circle of that radius with pull, fully at width pixels away. axis is required in every vortex and every around shape: three numbers, a direction of length 1 (between 0.999 and 1.001; the editor pre-fills [0, 0, 1], which is the plain turn on the screen). A vortex accelerates particles tangentially around the line through center along axis, and inner, outer, falloff and ring measure the distance to that line; an around shape places births on the circle of its radius that lies in the plane perpendicular to axis. Depth (z, towards the viewer) only moves with canvas.perspective: without it the z part of the axis is ignored and the particles see the axis projected on the screen. turbulence scrolls its noise speed pixels per second to the right, changes it time_scale times as fast and scales its x and y by mask. The turbulence force uses a periodic noise of 256 cells and the host wraps its phase, so it does not jump with the clock left on for days; with smooth and gyro, depth_parallax also follows the phone's tilt. noise_field picks one number u from 0 to 1 for each particle (the random number every particle draws when it is born) and gives it the strength s = strength.min + u·(strength.max − strength.min) (pixels per second squared, negative pushes the other way) and the phase φ = phase.min + u·(phase.max − phase.min) (pixels). It reads the noise at p = (x + φ + t·drift, y + φ + t·drift, z + φ + t·drift)·scale, t being the seconds of the clock of the system and drift in pixels per second, and accelerates the particle by s·mask·(N(p.x, p.y, p.z), N(p.z, p.x, p.y), N(p.y, p.z, p.x)), times its window, read once per simulation step at the position and clock of the start of the step and integrated as a plain acceleration: the step adds acceleration·step to the velocity (1/60 s live), with no damping or scaling of its own (the drag force and cap of the system act on the sum of the forces as always), and the position then moves by velocity·step; the third part goes into the speed in depth the same way. N is a 3D simplex noise of one octave over the standard table of 256 permutations perm: the point is skewed by 1/3 into the cells i, j and k of x, y and z and unskewed by 1/6, each of the four corners of its simplex adds (0.6 − d²)⁴ times the dot product of its offset d with one of the 12 edge gradients (nothing where d² is above 0.6), and the sum is multiplied by 32, so it stays within −1 and 1. The corner at cell offset (a, b, c) takes the gradient perm[i + a + perm[j + b + perm[k + c]]] % 12, all indices taken modulo 256, except the last corner (1, 1, 1), which takes it from perm[i + 1 + perm[j + 1 + perm[k]]] % 12. Unlike the curl of turbulence it has divergence, so particles gather where it converges. z is the depth with perspective, and the third part moves the depth; a system without perspective reads the noise at z = 0 and ignores the third part of mask. The noise repeats every 128 units along the diagonal, so the host wraps t·drift·scale by 128 and it does not jump with the clock left on for days. The GPU and the processor compute the same noise. oscillate picks a frequency, a scale and a phase for each particle (each from its own random number, or with random: "shared" all three from one) and moves it around where it would be without drifting (x and y a quarter of a turn apart, scaled by mask), fades its alpha by up to the scale or grows and shrinks its size by that fraction. cap limits the speed, torque adds degrees per second to the spin each second, spin_drag slows the spin and slow_near multiplies the speed every 1/60 s by factor near its target, fading to no effect between inner and outer.
  • Emitters. delay and duration count from the start of the system or from the last play(); a finished emitter, with no particle alive, lets the scene sleep. periodic alternates emission and pause for times picked from the seed. One-time bursts fire whenever the emitter starts: at first, after its delay, at each periodic window and after play(). max_per_step keeps the rest of the births for the next steps.
  • Children. static children emit on their own around the origin of their parent. spawn and death children start the one-time bursts of their emitters (one particle when they have none, at most 1000) where each particle is born or dies, in the same step, and follow children are born rate times a second at each living particle, ride along with it unless their system is world, and die with it. Shapes of children are placed around the particle, moved by offset and turned by angle; scale also scales their sizes. probability is drawn once per particle and max limits the children born in a step; at most 1024 births and 1024 deaths of a system start children in a step. points_from takes the control points of the parent. A child that is born from its parent (spawn, death or follow) cannot start particles from a layer.
  • Children in depth. A child of a system with perspective that has perspective of its own is born at the depth of its parent plus its own init.depth, and a follow child moves in depth with its parent; with velocity in inherit it also adds the speed in depth of its parent to its own init.depth_speed (and takes it every step with follow_inherit); a child without perspective is born where its parent is drawn, at f / (f + z) times its size and inherited speed, and a follow child moves as far as its parent is drawn to move.
