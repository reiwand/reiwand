# The newer fields

On this page: every newer field with its meaning and feature.

## Fields: space to shapes

| Field | Meaning | Feature |
|-------|---------|---------|
| `space` | `local` (prefilled) or `world`: in `world` the transform of the layer and of control points only places particles when they are born | `particles2` |
| `points` | at most 8 control points `{ "at" (may be bound), "follow": "cursor", "space", "angle" }`, `at` or `follow` | `particles2` |
| `lock` | settings layers may not change through `instance` (`alpha`, `size`, `count`, `speed`, `life`, `time_scale`, `brightness`, `color`, `points`) | `particles2` |
| emitter `duration`, `delay` | seconds after which the emitter stops (0 never) and before it starts (0 to 86400) | `particles2` |
| emitter `periodic` | `{ "on": [min, max], "off": [min, max] }` seconds of emission and pause, picked from the seed | `particles2` |
| emitter `max_per_step`, `audio_gate` | at most that many births per 1/60 s step (1 to 1000), and no births while the sound is silent | `particles2` |
| shape `box` | also `{ "from", "to", "inner": [w, h], "at" }`, a hollow box around a control point | `particles2` |
| shape `circle` | also `scale: [x, y]` (0.01 to 100), `sign: [x, y]` (−1, 0 or 1: half or quarter circles) and `at` | `particles2` |
| shape `layer` | `{ "id", "threshold", "color", "inherit", "jitter", "refresh": "static" \| "on_change" }` births where a layer is opaque, taking its color and motion | `particle_layers` |
| shape `around` | `{ "axis", "at", "count" (1 to 4096), "arc", "radius", "speed", "mode": "repeat" \| "mirror", "restart" }` births in turn around a point | `particle_fields` |
| shape `between` | `{ "from", "to", "count", "span", "mode", "arc": { "amount", "side" }, "taper": { "position", "speed", "size" } }` births along a line or arc between two points | `particle_fields` |
| `init` ranges | each range may add an exponent `[min, max, exponent]` (0.1 to 10) that leans the pick to one end; `color` too | `particles2` |
| `init.alpha`, `hsv`, `palette` | an opacity range of its own; `{ "hue": [0, 360], "steps", "sat", "val" }`; or `{ "colors" (1 to 10), "noise": [h, s, v] }` (not both), which replace `color` | `particles2` |
| `init.gradient` | at most 8 `[t, color]` stops in order, read at a random place (leaned by the exponent of `color`) and replacing `color`; not with `hsv` or `palette` | `particles2` |
| `init.velocity`, `radial`, `inherit` | `[[vx0, vy0], [vx1, vy1], exponent?]` replacing `speed` and `angle`; a speed away from the center of the shape; `{ "from" (cursor or control point), "speed": [min, max] }` | `particles2` |
| `init.turbulent`, `offset_noise`, `remap` | turbulent starting velocity `{ "forward", "scale", "noise_speed", "offset", "speed", "phase", "audio", "kind", "unit", "window", "reference", "hold_silence" }`; a fractal offset of the birth `{ "distance", "scale", "sign", "octaves" (1 to 6), "noise_scale", "noise_speed" }`; at most 8 `{ "input", "in", "clamp_in", "output", "out", "clamp_out", "op", "wave", "kind", "unit", "window", "reference", "hold_silence" }` that map `daytime`, `runtime`, `life`, `age`, `speed`, `random`, `distance:pN`, `audio:<band>` or `cursor:x\|y` onto `size`, `alpha`, `color`, `speed`, `life`, `rotation`, `spin` or `frame` with `set`, `mul`, `add` or `sub` and an optional `sine` or `noise` wave | `particle_fields` |
| `perspective`, `init.depth`, `init.depth_speed` | `{ "focal" (1 to 100000 px), "near" (1 to 100000 px, prefilled 1) }`, both required, and depth ranges in pixels, which need `perspective` | `particle_depth` |
| `init.tilt` | `{ "angle": [[xmin, ymin], [xmax, ymax]], "spin": [[xmin, ymin], [xmax, ymax]] }`, both required, in degrees and degrees per second (angle within ±3600, spin within ±36000). Each particle picks a start angle and a spin in the ranges for the turn about the horizontal (x) and the vertical (y) line of its own sprite, and the angle is `start + spin·age`. The sprite is drawn with its width times `\|cos y\|` and its height times `\|cos x\|` along its own directions, so it composes with `rotation`, `orient` and `angle` (the turn is applied after them, in the frame of the sprite, and a trail or stretch along the sprite shortens with the width); with `perspective` the near edge is drawn larger than the far one; `lit` and the normal map use the normal of the turned sprite, lit on both sides. The back of the sprite shows the same picture, not mirrored. The turn is computed in the shader from the particle, so it costs nothing on the simulation. `tilt` is always written: zero ranges (`[[0,0],[0,0]]` for both) mean no turn, any other value turns the sprite; a fixed angle is a range with equal ends | `particles2` (when something turns) |
| shape `sphere` | `{ "center": [x, y], "depth": z, "radius": [rx, ry, rz], "inner": f, "sign": [sx, sy, sz] }`, every field required and `at` (a control point) `null` when unused: births fill the volume of an ellipsoid evenly (the distance from the middle grows with the cube root of a uniform number and the direction is uniform in 3D), between the fraction `inner` (0 to 1, of the three radii; 0 is a full ball) and the edge, and `sign` (−1, 0 or 1 for each of x, y and z, `[0, 0, 0]` for both sides of each) keeps them on one side by mirroring. x and y give the position, z the depth. `push` (0 to 100000 px, may be absent; absent, `null` or 0 is no push) moves every birth that far outward along its own direction from the middle | `particle_depth`, and `particle_push` with `push` |

## Fields: forces to render brightness

| Field | Meaning | Feature |
|-------|---------|---------|
| force `window` | `[in_start, in_end, out_start, out_end]` fractions of the life, in order, in which any force fades in and out, along `x²(3 − 2x)` of the share `x` of each ramp | `particles2` |
| force `ramp` | next to `window`: `"smooth"` (the easing above, what a force without `ramp` does) or `"linear"` (the fade is `x` itself, a straight line from one end of the ramp to the other); may be absent | `window_ramp` |
| forces | `{ "oscillate": { "what": "position" \| "alpha" \| "size", "from": "top" \| "floor", "freq", "scale", "phase", "mask" } }`, `{ "cap": speed }`, `{ "torque": k }`, `{ "spin_drag": k }`, `{ "slow_near": { "target", "inner", "outer", "factor" } }`; `attractor`, `vortex` and `orbit` take `radius` and `falloff` (`constant`, `linear`, `inverse_square`), `vortex` also `inner`, `outer` and `ring: { "radius", "width", "pull" }`, `turbulence` also `speed`, `time_scale` and `mask` | `particles2` |
| forces | `{ "keep_distance": { "target", "distance", "strength" } }` (−1000 to 1000), `{ "keep_between": { "from", "to", "strength" } }`, `{ "remap": { … } }` applied every step (input `age` too) | `particle_steering` |
| force `noise_field` | `{ "strength": { "min", "max" }, "phase": { "min", "max" }, "scale" (0.000001 to 10), "drift", "mask": [x, y, z] (each 0 to 1) }`, every field written, strengths, phases and drift within ±100 000: a push along a 3D simplex noise at a strength of its own for each particle | `particle_noise` |
| `oscillate` `random` | `"separate"` (what an `oscillate` without it does) or `"shared"`: with `shared` the frequency, the scale and the phase of each particle come from one random number `r` in 0 to 1, frequency `freq[0] + r·(freq[1] − freq[0])`, scale (and the peak of `from: floor`) `scale[0] + r·(scale[1] − scale[0])`, and phase in turns `(phase[0] + r·(phase[1] − phase[0]))·frequency/2π`, so a particle that swings faster also swings wider and later; may be absent | `oscillate_random` |
| force `boids` | `{ "radius" (1 to 10000), "separation", "alignment", "cohesion" (0 to 10), "max_speed" }` flocking, for systems of at most 8192 particles | `boids` |
| collider `mask` | `{ "mask": { "texture", "threshold" } }`, the part of an image stretched over the canvas whose alpha is above `threshold` (prefilled 0.5), which particles bounce off or die on | `particles2` |
| colliders | `{ "box": { "from", "to", "inside" } }`, `{ "segment": { "from", "to" } }`, `circle` with `inside`, and `stick: true` | `particle_collisions` |
| collider `layer` | `{ "layer": { "id" } }`, the opaque shape of a layer | `particle_collisions` |
| `children` | at most 4 `{ "system", "on": "static" \| "spawn" \| "death" \| "follow", "probability", "max", "offset", "angle", "scale" (0 to 100), "points_from", "inherit", "follow_inherit" }`: other systems born with or from each particle, at most two levels deep and never back to themselves; `inherit` copies `color`, `size`, `alpha` or `velocity`, every step with `follow_inherit` on `follow` children; a collider that `kill`s counts as a death | `particle_children` |
| render `brightness`, `cycles`, `stretch` | 0 to 16, may be bound; repeats the frames that many times per life (1 to 64, with `frames: life`); `stretch` is `{ "mode", "depth", "time", "min", "max" }`, all required, `depth` being `flat` (the trail comes from the speed without perspective), `motion` (with `perspective` the stroke follows the velocity of the drawn position, `v = k·v_plane + (q − c)·(−ż)/(f + z)` with `q` the drawn position, `c` the centre, `f` the focal length, `z` the depth and `ż` its speed `init.depth_speed`: with `add` the length is the side plus `clamp(time·|v|, min, max)` pixels and with `relative` the side times `clamp(time·|v|, min, max)`, without `min` and `max` scaled by `k` because `v` already is on screen; the stroke points back along that motion, so a particle that only moves in depth is drawn as a stroke pointing away from the centre and longer the farther it is; without `perspective` it equals `flat`; it needs `particles2`) or `projected` (with `perspective` the trail is `clamp(time·|v|·k, min·k, max·k)` for `add`, `k` being the nearness of the particle, `focal / (focal + z)`, so it follows the motion seen on screen; `relative` already follows the projected side, so `depth` changes only `add`; a projected stretch needs `particles2`) or `segment` (the stretch is a stroke of `clamp(time·|v|, min, max)` pixels, or for `relative` that many times the side the particle has before the perspective, along its velocity in depth `v = (vx, vy, ż)`, centred on the particle, whose two ends are seen with the perspective of the system, each at its own depth: a star that comes towards the viewer draws a stroke along the line from the centre, shorter the farther it is, and the part of the stroke nearer than `near` is cut off; with `add` the length on screen is the side plus the distance between the two drawn ends and with `relative` that distance; without `perspective` it is the `flat` stroke centred on the particle; it needs `particles2`): with `mode: add` the length is the side plus `clamp(time·|v|, min, max)` pixels, and `min` equal to `max` only turns a sprite along its motion; with `mode: relative` it is the side times `clamp(time·|v|, min, max)`, `min` and `max` being multiples of the side, so it may be shorter than the side while the width stays the side (a relative stretch needs `particles2`). `oscillate` takes `from`: `top` gives `1 − s·(1 − w)` and `floor` gives `scale[0] + u·(scale[1] − scale[0])·w` with `u` random per particle in 0 to 1, a base that does not rise above the first scale value (`alpha` and `size` only; `position` takes `top`) | `particles2` |

## Fields: render stretch to shaders

| Field | Meaning | Feature |
|-------|---------|---------|
| render `stretch` `anchor` | `"tip"` (the front of the stroke stays on the particle and the trail runs back along the motion, what a stretch without `anchor` does) or `"centre"` (the stroked quad is centred on the particle: half of the stroke ahead of it and half behind, the length as given by `mode`); a `segment` stroke is always centred; may be absent | `stretch_anchor` |
| render `order` | `newest`, `oldest`, `largest`, `smallest` or `lowest`: the particles drawn on top; others are drawn in no set order | `particles2` |
| render `rope`, `trail` | `{ "subdivide" (0 to 7), "width", "uv_scale", "uv_scroll", "smooth" }` joins particles in birth order; trails of up to 64 steps with `segments`, `subdivide`, `uv_scale`, `uv_scroll` and `textured` | `particle_ropes` |
| render `cutout`, `orient`, `angle` | `[start, end, opacity]` remaps the alpha of the texture; `screen`, `velocity` or `fixed` with `angle` | `particle_looks` |
| render `lit`, `normal`, `refract` | `lit` lights the sprites with the scene lights (the `z` of a light lifts it toward the viewer, and a light behind a sprite lights its back); a spot with a `texture` casts its picture (image, video or layer) on them as it does on a lit layer, at the cost of one more texture read per lit pixel and spot with a picture, the same at every level; `normal` is a `linear` normal map laid over each sprite and turned with it; the `lighting` block of the layer, when set, lights them too and gives `strength` and `emissive`. `refract` bends what is behind the sprites by the normal map, and always states its `units` and its `color`: `{ "normal", "units": "size", "amount", "color" }` (0 to 1) bends up to `amount` times half the size of a sprite times the normal and the opacity, and `{ "normal", "units": "pixels", "shift", "color" }` (0 to 4096) bends up to `shift` canvas pixels times the normal and the opacity, the same for any sprite size and along the whole length of a trail, a rope or a stretched or non-square sprite (a thin long drop can bend the background 40 pixels); `amount` is refused with `pixels` and `shift` with `size`. `color` is `none` (the sprites are drawn only as the bend), `multiply` (the bent background is multiplied by the color of each sprite, its texture, `color_over_life` and the particle color, mixed by its opacity: a white sprite leaves the bend as it is, a red one tints it red; the opacity mixes only the color, the bent picture fills the whole sprite, so a translucent sprite bends less but nothing of the unbent background shows through it; a lower `amount` or `shift` makes it bend less), `add` (the color of the sprite times its opacity is added over the bend: a black sprite adds nothing) or `light` (the background behind the sprite stays unbent, and the bent background, multiplied by the color of each sprite, its texture, `color_over_life` and the particle color and by its opacity, is added over it as light: a black sprite leaves the background as it is and a white opaque one adds the whole bent picture, so a glass bubble glows with what it refracts instead of replacing it; overlapping sprites add their light, and in a layer whose systems mix `light` with the other colors, the `light` ones add their light after the others have bent and painted); trails, long trails and ropes paint their color the same way, with the shader of a particle shader when the layer has one. A scene without `color` is refused and the loader names the field; it works at full size at Q3, at half size at Q2 (the color is painted at full size over the half-size bend) and leaves the sprites out at Q1 and Q0, color included; a device without a blendable half-float target leaves it out too and names the layer in the notes of what was left out. Trails, long trails and ropes are lit and refract too, as a flat surface facing the viewer for the light and as a round tube across their width for the bend (their normal map is not read); sprites drawn with a particle shader take the light and the bend after their own `particle` function, with one more pipeline per shader and blend for each, built only when the layer is lit or refracts. A lit trail or rope costs the same light loop per pixel as a lit sprite, and a refracting one draws once more into the bend target at the size of the level, like the sprites | `particle_lighting` |
| render `shader` | a package effect with `stage: "particle"`; besides `fn particle`, its WGSL may define `fn pixel(p: Pixel) -> vec4<f32>` (see «Particle shaders») | `particle_shaders` |
